Melbourne Airport (IATA: **MEL**), also known as Tullamarine, is Australia's second-busiest airport and Victoria's main international gateway, serving more than 38 million passengers a year. It sits about 23 km northwest of central Melbourne.

## Overview

MEL operates four terminals connected by internal walkways rather than a train system — a notable gap for an airport of its size, since Melbourne remains one of the world's busiest airports without any rail link to the city center, despite a rail project having been discussed and delayed for years.

## Terminals

- **Terminal 1:** Qantas domestic flights.
- **Terminal 2:** International flights, including Qantas's international operations.
- **Terminal 3:** Virgin Australia domestic flights.
- **Terminal 4:** Budget domestic carriers including Jetstar.

All four terminals are connected by an internal walkway, making transfers straightforward on foot despite the lack of a train.

## Airlines

Qantas has a major presence at MEL for both domestic and international routes, alongside Jetstar for budget domestic travel and Virgin Australia as another significant domestic operator. MEL's role as Victoria's primary gateway makes it a key connecting point for both interstate and international travel.

## How to Get There

- **SkyBus:** **AU$24–26** one-way, running every 10 minutes and taking about 20–30 minutes direct to Southern Cross Station in the CBD — the fastest and most reliable public transport option, since there's no train.
- **Taxi:** Around **AU$65** plus a mandatory **AU$4.78 airport access fee**, about 25–30 minutes depending on traffic.
- **Uber:** Generally comparable in price to a taxi, about 25–30 minutes.

*Prices above are indicative and can change — always check current fares with [SkyBus](https://www.skybus.com.au/) or your ride-hailing app.*

## Getting Through Security and Passport Control

- **Domestic flights:** Security only. Typically **15–20 minutes** off-peak, up to **25–35 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control (or SmartGates for eligible passport holders). Typically **25–35 minutes** off-peak, up to **40–60 minutes** at peak.
- MEL is generally efficient by international standards, and SmartGates speed up processing considerably for eligible travelers on arrival.

## Parking

All four terminals have adjacent short- and long-stay car parks. Pre-booking online is notably cheaper than the walk-up rate, and off-airport parking options offer further savings for longer stays.

## Wifi

Free wifi is available across the terminals with a time-limited free session; an eSIM is a reliable backup for longer connectivity needs.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Domestic flights:** **90 minutes** before departure is typically enough.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** ahead, especially for evening long-haul departures from Terminal 2.
- Since there's no train link, always budget for potential road traffic delays on the Tullamarine Freeway when planning your journey to the airport, particularly during Melbourne's peak commute hours.

## Where to Eat

MEL reflects Melbourne's famous coffee and cafe culture well, with good coffee options and a solid mix of local and international dining spread across all four terminals, particularly strong in Terminal 2 and Terminal 4.

## Lounges

- **Qantas Club and Qantas International lounges** across Terminals 1, 2, and 3, accessible with eligible tickets, elite status, or membership.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available, mainly in Terminal 2.

## Duty-Free Shopping

MEL's duty-free selection includes Australian specialties — wine, opals, and Australian-made cosmetics and skincare — alongside the usual international fashion and electronics brands, concentrated in Terminal 2's international departures.

## Long Layover Tips

- With 5+ hours and a visa situation permitting entry, central Melbourne is reachable via SkyBus in 20–30 minutes — Melbourne's famous laneway cafes and street art are a short walk from Southern Cross Station.
- If staying airside, Terminal 2 has the best mix of lounges and dining for a long international wait.
- Since there's no train option, if you're on a tight connection to your onward city trip, build in extra buffer time for potential road traffic rather than assuming SkyBus timing is guaranteed.
