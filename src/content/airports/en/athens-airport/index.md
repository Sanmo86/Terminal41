Athens International Airport (IATA: **ATH**) is Greece's busiest airport and the primary hub of Aegean Airlines. ATH [handled a record 31.9 million passengers in 2024](https://www.travelandtourworld.com/news/article/aia-sets-passenger-record-in-2024-how-you-need-to-know/), a 13% jump on 2023 and 25% above its pre-pandemic 2019 total. It sits about 27 km east of central Athens.

## Overview

ATH operates a single integrated terminal and holds a genuinely unusual distinction: excavation work during the airport's construction uncovered real ancient artifacts, and rather than simply removing them, the airport built an actual on-site archaeological museum to display finds unearthed during its own construction — a fitting detail for the gateway to one of the world's most historically significant cities.

## Terminals

ATH operates around a single integrated terminal, handling both Schengen and international traffic, including the full network of Aegean Airlines and its Star Alliance partners.

## Airlines

Aegean Airlines, Greece's flag carrier, operates its principal hub at ATH, with an extensive network across Europe and the Greek islands as a member of Star Alliance. That network, combined with Athens' role as the primary gateway to the Greek islands, makes ATH one of the busiest and best-connected airports in southeastern Europe during peak tourist season.

## Getting There

- **Metro (Line 3):** **9 EUR**, a direct line to central Athens in about 40 minutes — the most reliable option regardless of traffic.
- **X95 bus:** **5.50 EUR**, running 24 hours, about 50–60 minutes.
- **Taxi:** A fixed rate of **35–40 EUR** to central Athens, about 40 minutes.

*Prices above are approximate and subject to change — always check current fares with [Athens Metro](https://www.stasy.gr/) or the official taxi rate board.*

## Security and Passport Control Wait Times

- **Schengen flights:** Security only for most intra-EU/Schengen routes. Typically **15–20 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **25–35 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **25–35 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **40–60 minutes** during peak international departure banks.
- ATH sees heavy seasonal variation tied to Greek island tourism — expect longer waits during the summer high season.

## Parking

The terminal has adjacent short- and long-stay parking, with clear signage for both options.

## Wifi

Free wifi is available in the terminal with a time-limited free session.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Schengen flights:** Arrive **2 hours** before departure.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** before departure, especially during peak summer departure periods.
- Given the significant seasonal tourism swing, add extra buffer time during the June–September high season.

## Where to Eat

ATH has a good selection of Greek cuisine alongside international chains, spread across the terminal. At the Sofitel, **Jenny's Rooftop Restaurant Bar** serves refined Greek cuisine with valley views, and **Mesoghaia** runs Greek and Mediterranean dishes around the clock.

## Airport Hotel

The **Sofitel Athens Airport** sits about 50 meters from the terminal — a genuine 2-minute walk from arrivals, no shuttle needed. Beyond Jenny's and Mesoghaia, it has an open-air terrace for a pleasant evening wait.

## Lounges

- The **Aegean Airlines Business Lounge** is available for eligible business class passengers, elite status holders, or paid access.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

ATH's duty-free area includes genuinely Greek specialties — olive oil and ouzo — alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Layover Tips

- With 4 hours or more and a visa situation that allows entry, central Athens and the Acropolis are reachable via Metro in about 40 minutes.
- If staying airside, don't skip the on-site archaeological museum — it's a genuinely unusual piece of airport history, built directly from what was found during construction.
- Given the heavy summer tourist season, book any onward transport or accommodation well ahead if traveling during the peak Greek island travel months.
