Christchurch International Airport (IATA: **CHC**) is the South Island's main airport and a key hub for Air New Zealand, with more than 6 million passengers a year. It sits about 10 km northwest of central Christchurch.

## Overview

CHC operates a single integrated terminal and holds a genuinely rare distinction: it is one of only a handful of gateway cities in the world used to launch flights to Antarctica. The airport hosts logistics operations for the United States Antarctic Program, New Zealand's Antarctica programme, and Italy's Antarctic program, with specialized flights departing directly to McMurdo Station and Scott Base during the austral summer.

## Terminals

CHC operates around a single integrated terminal complex, with domestic and international sections connected internally, handling Air New Zealand's extensive domestic network alongside international carriers serving Australia, the Pacific, and beyond.

## Airlines

Air New Zealand operates a key hub at CHC, running an extensive domestic network across the South Island and beyond alongside international routes to Australia and the Pacific. That network, combined with Christchurch's role as the primary logistics gateway to the South Island, makes CHC an essential connecting point for travel around New Zealand's South Island.

## Getting There

- **Metro bus:** **NZD 8.50**, a direct bus service to central Christchurch, about 25 minutes.
- **Taxi:** Typically **NZD 45–55** to the city center, about 15–20 minutes.
- **Uber:** Generally comparable in price to a taxi, about 15–20 minutes.

*Prices above are approximate and subject to change — always check current fares with [Metro](https://www.metroinfo.co.nz/) or your rideshare app.*

## Security and Passport Control Wait Times

- **Domestic flights:** Security only. Typically **10–15 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **20–30 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **20–30 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **35–50 minutes** during peak international departure banks.
- Given CHC's more moderate scale, processing is generally quick even during busier periods.

## Parking

The terminal has adjacent short- and long-stay parking, with clear signage for both options.

## Wifi

Free, unlimited wifi is available throughout the airport with no time limit.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Domestic flights:** **90 minutes** before departure is usually sufficient.
- **International flights:** Arrive **2.5 hours** before departure, especially during peak international departure periods.
- Given CHC's smaller scale compared to Auckland, these times are generally more than sufficient outside of peak hours.

## Where to Eat

CHC has a good selection of New Zealand cuisine alongside international chains, spread across the terminal.

## Lounges

- The **Air New Zealand Regional Lounge** is available for eligible business class passengers, elite status holders, or paid access.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

CHC's duty-free area includes New Zealand wine and manuka honey alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Layover Tips

- With 3 hours or more and a visa situation that allows entry, central Christchurch is reachable via Metro bus or taxi in about 15–25 minutes.
- If staying airside, look for the Antarctic-themed displays around the terminal — a nod to Christchurch's genuinely unusual role as one of the world's few real gateway cities to Antarctica.
- Given Christchurch's role as the main gateway to the South Island, this airport is often a smoother and less congested alternative to Auckland if your itinerary allows routing through here instead.
