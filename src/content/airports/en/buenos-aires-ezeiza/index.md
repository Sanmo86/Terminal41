Ministro Pistarini International Airport (IATA: **EZE**), commonly known as Ezeiza, is Argentina's main international gateway and the primary long-haul hub of Aerolíneas Argentinas, with more than 11 million passengers a year. It sits about 35 km southwest of central Buenos Aires.

## Overview

EZE operates as Buenos Aires's international airport — and understanding that distinction matters: Buenos Aires is one of the relatively few major cities that splits its air traffic across two separate airports. Ezeiza handles nearly all international long-haul flights, while Aeroparque Jorge Newbery (AEP), a much smaller airport right in the city center, handles most domestic and regional flights. Booking the wrong one is a genuinely common and costly mistake for travelers unfamiliar with the city.

## Terminals

EZE operates around Terminal A, the main international terminal, handling Aerolíneas Argentinas' long-haul network alongside other international carriers serving Buenos Aires.

## Airlines

Aerolíneas Argentinas, the national carrier, operates its principal international hub at EZE, with long-haul routes to Europe, North America, and other destinations in Latin America, alongside a wide range of international carriers connecting Buenos Aires to the rest of the world. Most of the airline's domestic network, by contrast, operates out of Aeroparque (AEP) instead.

## Getting There

- **Tienda León shuttle bus:** **15–20 USD**, direct to central Buenos Aires, about 45 minutes — the most popular and reliable option.
- **Remis / official taxi:** **35–45 USD** via the official counter, about 40–50 minutes depending on traffic. Prices here are often quoted in USD given the Argentine peso's significant volatility.
- **Uber:** Generally comparable in price to a remis, about 40–50 minutes.

*Prices above are approximate and subject to change — Argentina's currency has historically been volatile, so always check current fares with [Tienda León](https://www.tiendaleon.com.ar/) or your rideshare app before booking.*

## Security and Passport Control Wait Times

- **Regional flights:** Security plus immigration for most South American routes. Typically **20–30 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **35–50 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **25–40 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **45–65 minutes** during peak international departure banks.
- EZE handles a high volume of long-haul departures — budget extra time during Aerolíneas Argentinas' peak banks.

## Parking

The terminal has adjacent short- and long-stay parking, with clear signage.

## Wifi

Free wifi is available in the terminal with a time-limited free session.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Regional flights:** Arrive **2 hours** before departure.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** before departure, especially during Aerolíneas Argentinas' peak long-haul departure banks.
- Double-check which of Buenos Aires's two airports your flight actually uses before planning your transfer — EZE and AEP are roughly 40 km apart.

## Where to Eat

EZE has a good selection of Argentine cuisine alongside international chains, spread across the terminal.

## Lounges

- The **Aerolíneas Argentinas VIP Lounge** is available for eligible business class passengers, elite status holders, or paid access.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

EZE's duty-free area includes a strong selection of Argentine wine and leather goods alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Layover Tips

- With 6 hours or more and a visa situation that allows entry, central Buenos Aires is reachable via the Tienda León shuttle in about 45 minutes.
- If staying airside, EZE's lounges and dining reflect Argentina's strong culinary reputation — worth exploring even on a shorter connection.
- If your onward trip involves a domestic flight, confirm well in advance whether it departs from EZE or from Aeroparque (AEP) — transferring between the two takes over an hour and is a common trip-planning mistake.
