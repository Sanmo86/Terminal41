Milan Malpensa Airport (IATA: **MXP**) is Milan's main international gateway and easyJet's single largest base anywhere outside the United Kingdom, with more than 28 million passengers a year. It sits about 50 km northwest of central Milan.

## Overview

MXP operates two terminals and holds a genuinely notable distinction in European low-cost aviation: it's easyJet's biggest operating base anywhere in the world outside the UK, larger than any of the airline's home bases. Combined with Milan's global standing as a fashion capital, that gives MXP an unusual mix — a major budget-carrier hub with a duty-free shopping scene that punches well above its weight for luxury goods.

## Terminals

- **Terminal 1:** The main terminal, handling most full-service international carriers and Star Alliance partners.
- **Terminal 2:** easyJet's dedicated base terminal, connected to Terminal 1 by shuttle bus.

## Airlines

ITA Airways, Italy's flag carrier, operates a secondary hub at MXP alongside its primary base in Rome. easyJet's presence is the real story here, though — Malpensa is the airline's largest base anywhere outside the UK, running an extensive network of budget routes across Europe from a dedicated terminal.

## Getting There

- **Malpensa Express:** **~13 EUR**, a direct train to Milano Centrale, about 50 minutes.
- **Malpensa Shuttle bus:** **~10 EUR**, frequent departures to Milano Centrale, about 50–60 minutes.
- **Taxi:** A fixed rate of **~95 EUR** to central Milan, about 50 minutes.

*Prices above are approximate and subject to change — always check current fares with [Trenord](https://www.malpensaexpress.it/) or your taxi option.*

## Security and Passport Control Wait Times

- **Schengen flights:** Security only for most intra-EU/Schengen routes. Typically **15–20 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **25–35 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **20–30 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **35–50 minutes** during peak international departure banks.
- MXP's two-terminal split means easyJet's high-volume departures at Terminal 2 can see notably busier security lines than Terminal 1 during peak morning and evening waves.

## Parking

Both terminals have extensive short- and long-stay parking, with clear signage. Booking online ahead of arrival is cheaper than the walk-up rate.

## Wifi

Free, unlimited wifi is available throughout the airport with no time limit.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Schengen flights:** Arrive **2 hours** before departure.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** before departure, especially during peak departure periods.
- Given MXP's distance from central Milan, factor in real transfer time — the trip itself takes 50 minutes or more even by the fastest train.

## Where to Eat

MXP has a strong selection of Italian cuisine and proper espresso bars alongside international chains, spread across both terminals.

## Lounges

- The **SEA Prime Lounge** is available for eligible business class passengers, elite status holders, or paid access.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

MXP's duty-free area reflects Milan's status as a global fashion capital — a genuinely strong selection of Italian fashion brands and wine alongside the usual international cosmetics and electronics.

## Layover Tips

- With 5 hours or more and a visa situation that allows entry, central Milan and the Duomo are reachable via Malpensa Express in about 50 minutes.
- If staying airside, MXP's duty-free fashion selection is genuinely worth a browse even without buying — it reflects Milan's global design reputation.
- Given the distance between terminals, confirm which one your connecting flight departs from — easyJet's Terminal 2 operations are functionally separate from Terminal 1's full-service carriers.
