Munich Airport (IATA: **MUC**) is Germany's second-busiest airport and Lufthansa's second-largest hub, handling more than 40 million passengers a year. It sits about 40 km (25 mi) northeast of central Munich, in the Bavarian countryside.

## Overview

MUC operates two terminals connected by a central area known as MAC (Munich Airport Center), which includes shops, restaurants, and — genuinely uniquely — the airport's own working brewery. As Lufthansa's second hub after Frankfurt, MUC anchors an extensive network across Europe and long-haul routes worldwide, with a reputation as one of Europe's more relaxed, efficient major airports.

## Terminals

- **Terminal 1:** A mix of international carriers, arranged in modules (A through E).
- **Terminal 2:** Lufthansa's main terminal and its Star Alliance partners, including a dedicated satellite building for additional gates.

The Munich Airport Center (MAC) sits between the two terminals and includes the Airbräu brewery, shops, and services accessible to both.

## Airlines

Lufthansa operates its second-largest hub at MUC, anchoring Terminal 2 with an extensive short-haul European network and a growing long-haul portfolio. Star Alliance partners add further connectivity, making MUC an efficient alternative to Frankfurt for many European and intercontinental routes.

## How to Get There

- **S-Bahn (S1/S8):** **€13.60** for a single ticket covering the full XXL zone, running every 10 minutes and taking about 40–45 minutes direct to Munich Hauptbahnhof (main station) — the most reliable option regardless of traffic.
- **Lufthansa Express Bus:** Around **€10.50**, about 45 minutes to Munich Hauptbahnhof, a comfortable alternative to the train.
- **Taxi:** With taximeter, typically **€70–90** to the city center, about 35–45 minutes depending on traffic — notably pricier than most European airport taxis given the distance.

*Prices above are indicative and can change — always check current fares with [Deutsche Bahn](https://www.bahn.com/en) or the [Munich Airport transport page](https://www.munich-airport.com/).*

## Getting Through Security and Passport Control

- **Schengen flights:** Security only, no passport control. Typically **15–25 minutes** off-peak, up to **30–40 minutes** during busy morning and evening departure waves.
- **International (non-Schengen) flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **25–40 minutes** off-peak, but can reach **50–70 minutes** during Lufthansa's peak connecting banks in Terminal 2.
- MUC is generally regarded as one of the calmer major European hubs for security, though Terminal 2's Lufthansa connecting waves can still get busy.

## Parking

Both terminals have adjacent short- and long-stay car parks, plus more affordable off-site options connected by shuttle. Pre-booking online is cheaper than the walk-up rate.

## Wifi

Free, unlimited wifi is available across both terminals with no time limit.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Schengen flights:** **90 minutes** before departure is typically enough outside peak hours.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** ahead, especially for Lufthansa's long-haul departures during peak connecting banks in Terminal 2.
- Munich's distance from the city means road and rail delays can eat into your buffer more than at a closer-in airport — build in extra time for the outbound trip.

## Where to Eat

MUC has a strong lineup of genuine Bavarian cuisine — pretzels, weisswurst, and hearty regional dishes — alongside international chains, with the standout being the airport's own Airbräu brewery restaurant and beer garden in the MAC area.

## Lounges

- **Lufthansa Business and Senator lounges** are spread across Terminal 2, reflecting Lufthansa's major hub presence, accessible with eligible tickets, elite status, or membership.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available in Terminal 1.

## Duty-Free Shopping

MUC's duty-free selection leans into German specialties — beer, precision watches, and chocolate — alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Long Layover Tips

- With 6+ hours and a Schengen-eligible passport, central Munich is reachable via S-Bahn in about 40 minutes, making Marienplatz and the old town realistically visitable.
- If staying airside, don't skip the Airbräu brewery in the Munich Airport Center — genuinely one of the more memorable airport experiences anywhere, whether or not you're connecting through the MAC area specifically.
- Given MUC's distance from the city, build real buffer time into any round trip if you're leaving the airport on a layover — the S-Bahn ride alone is 40+ minutes each way.
