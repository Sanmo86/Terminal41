Mariscal Sucre International Airport (IATA: **UIO**) is Ecuador's main gateway alongside Guayaquil, with more than 5 million passengers a year. It sits about 18 km east of central Quito, high in the Andes.

## Overview

UIO operates a single terminal and offers one of the more dramatic arrivals in South American aviation: the airport sits at more than 2,400 meters above sea level, surrounded by snow-capped volcanoes visible on a clear day. For many international travelers, UIO also functions as a key connecting point on the way to the Galápagos Islands, one of the world's most celebrated wildlife destinations.

## Terminals

UIO operates around a single terminal, handling both regional and international traffic, including Ecuador's main international carriers.

## Airlines

LATAM Airlines operates an extensive network from UIO, connecting Ecuador to destinations across South America, North America, and Europe. Avianca and other carriers also serve Quito directly, making it one of the best-connected gateways in the northern Andes region, alongside its role as a stepping stone to the Galápagos.

## Getting There

- **Aeroservicios shuttle bus:** **8 USD**, a direct service to central Quito, about 45 minutes.
- **Taxi:** Typically **25–35 USD** via the fixed-rate counter inside arrivals, about 40–45 minutes given the airport's distance from the city.
- **Uber:** Generally comparable in price to a taxi, about 40–45 minutes.

*Prices above are approximate and subject to change — always check current fares with the shuttle counter or your rideshare app.*

## Security and Passport Control Wait Times

- **Regional flights:** Security plus immigration for most South American routes. Typically **15–25 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **25–40 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **25–35 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **40–60 minutes** during peak international departure banks.
- UIO generally processes passengers efficiently despite significant tourist traffic tied to Galápagos connections.

## Parking

The terminal has adjacent short- and long-stay parking, with clear signage.

## Wifi

Free wifi is available in the terminal with a time-limited free session.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Regional flights:** Arrive **2 hours** before departure.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** before departure, especially during peak international departure periods.
- If connecting onward to the Galápagos, note that inter-island flights typically depart from Quito or Guayaquil separately — confirm your full itinerary in advance.

## Where to Eat

UIO has a good selection of Ecuadorian cuisine alongside international chains, spread across the terminal.

## Lounges

- The **LATAM VIP Lounge** is available for eligible business class passengers, elite status holders, or paid access.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

UIO's duty-free area includes genuinely Ecuadorian specialties — chocolate and Panama hats (which, despite the name, originate in Ecuador) — alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Layover Tips

- With 5 hours or more and a visa situation that allows entry, central Quito's UNESCO-listed historic center is reachable via shuttle bus or taxi in about 40–45 minutes.
- If staying airside, take a moment to notice the volcano views on a clear day — genuinely one of the most dramatic airport settings in South America.
- If your onward trip is to the Galápagos Islands, confirm well in advance whether you're flying via Quito or Guayaquil, and factor in the connection separately from your international arrival.
