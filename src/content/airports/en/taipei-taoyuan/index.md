Taiwan Taoyuan International Airport (IATA: **TPE**) is Taiwan's main international gateway and the primary hub of both China Airlines and EVA Air, with more than 45 million passengers a year. It sits about 40 km southwest of central Taipei.

## Overview

TPE operates two terminals and is consistently rated among the most passenger-friendly airports in Asia, thanks to a genuinely unusual amenity: a free cinema built into the terminal, alongside quiet reading rooms and other dedicated leisure spaces designed specifically for travelers with time to spend rather than just retail and dining.

## Terminals

TPE operates Terminal 1 and Terminal 2, connected by a Skytrain, handling the combined networks of China Airlines and EVA Air alongside a wide range of international carriers.

## Airlines

China Airlines and EVA Air, Taiwan's two major carriers, both operate extensive hubs at TPE, together covering a wide network across Asia, North America, Europe, and Oceania. That dual-hub structure makes TPE one of the best-connected airports in East Asia, particularly for travel between Asia and North America.

## Getting There

- **Taoyuan Airport MRT:** **160 TWD**, a direct line to Taipei Main Station in about 35–40 minutes — fast and reliable.
- **Taxi:** Metered, typically **1000–1200 TWD** via the official taxi rank, about 40–50 minutes.
- **Ubus (airport bus):** **90–145 TWD**, the cheapest option, about 50–60 minutes.

*Prices above are approximate and subject to change — always check current fares with the [Taoyuan Airport MRT](https://www.tymetro.com.tw/) or the official taxi rank.*

## Security and Passport Control Wait Times

- **Regional flights:** Security plus immigration for most nearby Asian routes. Typically **15–25 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **25–40 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **25–35 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **40–60 minutes** during peak international departure banks.
- TPE handles a high volume of both China Airlines and EVA Air traffic — budget extra time during their overlapping peak banks.

## Parking

Both terminals have extensive short- and long-stay parking, with clear signage.

## Wifi

Free, unlimited wifi is available throughout the airport with no time limit.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Regional flights:** Arrive **2 hours** before departure.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** before departure, especially during China Airlines' and EVA Air's peak departure banks.
- If connecting between T1 and T2, the Skytrain is fast, but budget a little extra time regardless.

## Where to Eat

TPE has an excellent selection of Taiwanese cuisine, including a genuine night-market-style food court, alongside international chains, spread across both terminals.

## Lounges

- The **China Airlines Dynasty Lounge** is available for eligible business class passengers, elite status holders, or paid access.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

TPE's duty-free area includes genuinely Taiwanese specialties — tea and pineapple cakes — alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Layover Tips

- With 5 hours or more and a visa situation that allows entry, central Taipei is reachable via the Airport MRT in about 35–40 minutes.
- If staying airside, don't skip the free cinema and reading rooms — genuinely one of the best ways to spend a long layover of any airport in Asia.
- Given the dual China Airlines/EVA Air hub structure, confirm which terminal your flight actually departs from — the Skytrain between T1 and T2 is fast, but it's still worth building in a buffer.
