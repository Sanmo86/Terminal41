São Paulo–Guarulhos International Airport (IATA: **GRU**) is Brazil's busiest airport and LATAM Airlines' largest hub, handling more than 43 million passengers a year. It sits about 25 km (16 mi) northeast of central São Paulo.

## Overview

GRU operates three terminals and functions as the primary gateway between South America and the rest of the world — more international routes connect through GRU than through any other Brazilian airport. A standout feature for travelers: a hotel is built directly into the airport complex, connected to Terminal 3 by a covered walkway, a genuinely convenient option for long layovers or early departures.

## Terminals

- **Terminal 1:** A mix of domestic and some international flights.
- **Terminal 2:** Additional domestic and international carriers.
- **Terminal 3:** LATAM Airlines' main hub terminal and the primary international gateway, connected to the on-site GRU Airport Hotel.

## Airlines

LATAM Airlines operates its largest hub at GRU, anchoring Terminal 3 with an extensive domestic network across Brazil plus international routes throughout South America, North America, Europe, and beyond as a Star Alliance-adjacent partner. That hub concentration makes GRU the best-connected airport for travel within Brazil and one of the primary gateways to South America overall.

## How to Get There

- **Airport bus (EMTU):** **R$ 15–45** depending on the route, with multiple lines covering different parts of São Paulo, about 45–60 minutes.
- **Taxi:** **R$ 90–130** to central São Paulo, about 40–60 minutes depending on traffic.
- **Uber:** Generally comparable in price to a taxi, about 40–60 minutes — widely used and reliable in São Paulo.

*Prices above are indicative and can change — always check current fares with [EMTU](https://www.emtu.sp.gov.br/) or your ride-hailing app.*

## Getting Through Security and Passport Control

- **Domestic flights:** Security only. Typically **20–30 minutes** off-peak, up to **35–50 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **30–45 minutes** off-peak, up to **50–70 minutes** during peak international departure banks — GRU's role as LATAM's largest hub means significant connecting traffic throughout the day.
- Build extra time around LATAM's major connecting banks, particularly for overnight departures to Europe and North America.

## Parking

GRU has parking available at all three terminals, with clear signage for short- and long-stay options.

## Wifi

Free, unlimited wifi is available throughout the airport with no time limit.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Domestic flights:** Arrive **2 hours** before departure.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** ahead, especially for LATAM's peak international departure banks.
- São Paulo's traffic is notoriously heavy at rush hour — budget significant extra buffer time for the drive to the airport during peak commute hours.

## Where to Eat

GRU has a solid selection of Brazilian regional cuisine — including proper churrasco — alongside international chains, spread across all three terminals.

## Lounges

- **LATAM VIP Lounges** are available in Terminal 3 for eligible business class passengers, elite status holders, or paid access.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

GRU's duty-free zone includes genuine Brazilian specialties — coffee and cachaça — alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Long Layover Tips

- With 6+ hours and a visa situation permitting entry, central São Paulo is reachable by bus or taxi in about 45–60 minutes.
- If staying airside, the connected GRU Airport Hotel makes a genuinely long overnight layover comfortable without leaving the airport complex.
- Given São Paulo's traffic, if you're heading into the city on a tight connection, build in real buffer time for the return trip.
