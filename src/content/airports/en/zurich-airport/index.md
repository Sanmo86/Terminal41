Zurich Airport (IATA: **ZRH**) is Switzerland's busiest airport and SWISS's principal hub, handling more than 31 million passengers a year. It sits just 10 km (6 mi) north of central Zurich, with an unusually fast and direct rail connection built right into the terminal complex.

## Overview

ZRH operates two main terminals plus a midfield dock connected by the automated SkyMetro train. As SWISS's main hub and a Star Alliance gateway, it anchors an extensive European network plus long-haul routes worldwide. The airport is consistently ranked among Europe's most efficient, reflecting Switzerland's broader reputation for punctuality and precision.

## Terminals

- **Terminal 1 & 2:** The main terminal complex, handling the bulk of SWISS and Star Alliance flights.
- **Midfield Dock (Terminal E):** A separate satellite building connected by the SkyMetro automated train, used for many long-haul departures.

## Airlines

SWISS operates its principal hub at ZRH, anchoring an extensive short-haul European network plus long-haul routes to North America, Asia, and beyond as a Star Alliance member. That hub concentration, combined with Zurich's central European location, makes ZRH one of the continent's most efficient connecting points.

## How to Get There

- **Train (SBB):** **CHF 7** for a standard ticket, with trains departing every few minutes directly beneath the terminal, about 10 minutes to Zürich Hauptbahnhof (main station) — genuinely one of the fastest airport rail links in Europe.
- **Taxi:** With taximeter, typically **CHF 50–70** to the city center, about 20 minutes.
- **Uber:** Generally comparable in price to a taxi, about 20 minutes, widely used in Zurich.

*Prices above are indicative and can change — always check current fares with [SBB](https://www.sbb.ch/en) or your ride-hailing app.*

## Getting Through Security and Passport Control

- **Schengen flights:** Security only, no passport control. Typically **15–25 minutes** off-peak, up to **30–40 minutes** during busy morning and evening waves.
- **International (non-Schengen) flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **25–40 minutes** off-peak, up to **45–65 minutes** during SWISS's peak connecting banks.
- ZRH is generally regarded as one of the most efficient and punctual major European airports, reflecting the broader Swiss reputation for precision.

## Parking

Both terminals have adjacent short- and long-stay car parks. Pre-booking online is cheaper than the walk-up rate.

## Wifi

Free, unlimited wifi is available across the airport with no time limit.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Schengen flights:** **90 minutes** before departure is typically enough outside peak hours.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** ahead, especially for SWISS's long-haul departures during peak connecting banks.
- Given the airport's efficiency, you generally don't need the extra buffer some other major European hubs require — but still allow more time during Zurich's busiest connecting waves.

## Where to Eat

ZRH has a strong selection of genuine Swiss cuisine alongside international chains, reflecting the country's high standards for food quality even in a transit environment.

## Lounges

- **SWISS Business and First Class lounges** are spread across the terminal complex, reflecting SWISS's major hub presence, accessible with eligible tickets, elite status, or membership.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

ZRH's duty-free selection leans into Swiss specialties — precision watches and chocolate — alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Long Layover Tips

- With 4+ hours and a Schengen-eligible passport, central Zurich is reachable via train in about 10 minutes, making a walk along the Limmat river or through the old town genuinely realistic even on a shorter layover.
- If staying airside, both terminals have a solid mix of lounges and Swiss-quality dining for a longer wait.
- Given the fast, frequent train connection, ZRH is one of the easier major European hubs to combine with a genuine city visit on even a moderate layover.
