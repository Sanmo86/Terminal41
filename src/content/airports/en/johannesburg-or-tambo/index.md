O.R. Tambo International Airport (IATA: **JNB**) is Africa's busiest airport by passenger traffic, handling more than 20 million travelers a year as South Africa's main international gateway. It sits about 24 km northeast of central Johannesburg and serves as the primary hub for South African Airways and a major connecting point for flights across the African continent.

## Overview

JNB operates as a single large terminal complex divided into domestic and international sections, rather than separate terminal buildings — a more straightforward layout than many similarly-sized international hubs. As Africa's leading aviation hub, it connects a huge number of routes across the continent alongside long-haul international services.

## Terminals

JNB's single terminal complex is split into two main areas:
- **Domestic Terminal:** Flights within South Africa and to some neighboring countries.
- **International Terminal:** Long-haul and regional international flights, including South African Airways' network across Africa, Europe, Asia, and the Americas.

## Airlines

South African Airways is JNB's flagship carrier and a key hub airline, alongside Airlink, which operates an extensive regional network connecting Johannesburg to destinations across Southern and East Africa. JNB is also a major arrival and departure point for international carriers connecting Africa to Europe, the Middle East, Asia, and the Americas.

## How to Get There

- **Gautrain:** **R210** with a Gautrain Gold Card (around R200–220 standard), a fast, modern, and widely recommended option — about 15 minutes to Sandton, or around 35 minutes to Park Station in central Johannesburg.
- **Official Taxi Rank:** **R300–R450** to central areas via the official metered taxi rank inside the airport, about 25–40 minutes depending on traffic and destination.
- **Uber / Bolt:** Generally comparable to or cheaper than a taxi, with the added benefit of GPS tracking and cashless payment, about 25–40 minutes.

*Prices above are indicative and can change — always check current fares with [Gautrain](https://www.gautrain.co.za/) or your ride-hailing app.*

## Getting Through Security and Passport Control

- **Domestic flights:** Security only. Typically **15–25 minutes** off-peak, up to **30–40 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **25–40 minutes** off-peak, up to **50–70 minutes** during peak international departure banks.
- As Africa's busiest connecting hub, JNB handles a significant volume of transfer passengers — if you're connecting internationally, follow the transfer signage carefully, as processes can vary depending on your onward destination.

## Parking

JNB has extensive parking directly at the terminal, with clear signage for both short- and long-stay options. Pre-booking online is generally cheaper than the walk-up rate.

## Wifi

Free wifi is available across the terminal with a time-limited free session; an eSIM with a South African data plan is a reliable backup, particularly useful for ride-hailing apps.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Domestic flights:** **90 minutes** before departure is generally sufficient.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** ahead, especially for evening departures during peak connecting banks.
- As with transport into the city, stick to well-lit, official areas of the terminal and avoid unofficial approaches from anyone offering "help" with luggage or transport — a common-sense precaution recommended for JNB specifically.

## Where to Eat

JNB has a good selection of South African cuisine — biltong, boerewors, and other local specialties — alongside international chains, concentrated mainly in the international terminal area.

## Lounges

- **Bidvest Premier Lounges** and other airline-specific lounges are available for eligible passengers with business class tickets, elite status, or paid access.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

JNB's duty-free area features South African specialties — wine, biltong, and local crafts and jewelry — alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands, making it a solid last stop for South African souvenirs.

## Long Layover Tips

- With 5+ hours and a visa situation that allows entry, Sandton is reachable via Gautrain in about 15 minutes — a good option for a short, safe visit given the direct rail connection.
- If staying airside, the international terminal has the better mix of lounges and dining for a longer wait.
- Given the specific transport safety guidance for JNB, plan your onward transport (Gautrain or a pre-booked ride) before you land, rather than figuring it out on the spot.
