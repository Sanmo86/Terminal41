Singapore Changi Airport (IATA: **SIN**) is consistently rated one of the best airports in the world, and for good reason — it combines major hub connectivity with genuinely enjoyable amenities most airports don't even attempt. It's the home base of Singapore Airlines and a key gateway between Europe, Australia, and the rest of Asia.

## Overview

Changi operates four terminals connected by Skytrain (T1–T3) and shuttle bus (T4), plus **Jewel Changi**, a separate landside complex attached near Terminal 1 that's become an attraction in its own right — accessible to visitors even without a flight, though transit passengers would need to clear immigration into Singapore to visit it.

## Terminals

- **Terminal 1:** A mix of international carriers, connected directly to Jewel.
- **Terminal 2:** Singapore Airlines and SilkAir/Scoot regional flights, alongside other carriers (undergoing periodic renovation in phases).
- **Terminal 3:** Singapore Airlines' primary long-haul terminal, with an indoor Butterfly Garden.
- **Terminal 4:** A standalone building with a more boutique feel, connected to the rest of the airport by free shuttle bus rather than Skytrain.

## Airlines

Singapore Airlines dominates Changi as its home hub, alongside its budget subsidiary Scoot. Changi is also a major stopover point for long-haul flights between Europe/the Middle East and Australia/New Zealand, hosting a wide range of international carriers across all four terminals.

## How to Get There

- **MRT Train:** **SGD 2–3** using a stored-value card (EZ-Link, NETS FlashPay, or contactless bank card), about 40 minutes into the city. Note the MRT doesn't run 24 hours — service runs roughly 5:31am to midnight.
- **Taxi:** Metered, typically **SGD 28–45** to the city center during the day (higher after midnight), about 20–30 minutes. Surcharges apply: an airport pickup surcharge (SGD 6–8 depending on time), a peak-hour surcharge (25%, weekdays 6–9:30am and 6pm–midnight), and a midnight surcharge (50%, midnight–6am).
- **Grab:** Singapore and Southeast Asia's dominant ride-hailing app, pricing generally comparable to a metered taxi, about 20–30 minutes.

*Prices above are indicative and can change — always check current fares with [Grab](https://www.grab.com/sg/) or [Changi Airport's official transport page](https://www.changiairport.com/).*

## Getting Through Security and Passport Control

- **Regional (ASEAN) flights:** Typically **15–20 minutes** off-peak, up to **25–35 minutes** during busy periods.
- **Long-haul international flights:** Typically **20–30 minutes** off-peak, up to **35–50 minutes** at peak — even at its busiest, Changi is known for moving passengers through efficiently compared to many similarly-sized hubs, part of why it's so highly rated.
- Automated immigration lanes are available for many passport holders, which helps keep queues moving even during busy periods.

## Parking

All terminals have adjacent short- and long-stay car parks, well-signed and generally straightforward given Changi's overall efficiency. Rates and current promotions are best checked directly with Changi Airport Group.

## Wifi

Free, unlimited wifi (WiFi@Changi) is available throughout the airport with no time cap.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Regional flights:** **90 minutes** before departure is typically enough.
- **Long-haul international flights:** **2.5 hours** ahead is generally sufficient — Changi's efficient processing means it rarely requires the full 3 hours recommended at some other major hubs.
- If you have time before your flight, build in extra time deliberately to enjoy Jewel or the terminal gardens — it's one of the few airports genuinely worth arriving early for.

## Where to Eat

Changi is known for excellent hawker-style food courts offering authentic Singaporean dishes — laksa, chicken rice, satay — alongside international chains and higher-end restaurants. Food quality and value are consistently praised, a rarity for airport dining.

## Lounges

- **Singapore Airlines' SilverKris Lounges**, including a flagship in Terminal 3, are among the most highly rated airline lounges in the world for eligible passengers.
- Numerous **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are available across all terminals.

## Duty-Free Shopping

Changi's duty-free selection is extensive and consistently ranked among the world's best, covering everything from local specialties to luxury fashion, electronics, and liquor — helped by Singapore's status as a major shopping destination in its own right.

## Long Layover Tips

- With 5.5+ hours on select tickets, Changi's free city tour program lets eligible transit passengers see highlights of Singapore at no cost — check eligibility at the transit visitor centers.
- If staying airside, Jewel is accessible from the public area near Terminal 1 (transit passengers need to clear Singapore immigration first) — the Rain Vortex, gardens, and shops are worth the visit even on a shorter layover.
- Changi is genuinely comfortable for overnight layovers, with 24-hour amenities, free movie theaters, and rest areas throughout the terminals — one of the easier airports in the world to spend the night in.
