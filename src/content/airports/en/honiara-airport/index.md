Honiara International Airport (IATA: **HIR**) is the Solomon Islands' main gateway and the hub of Solomon Airlines, with more than 350,000 passengers a year. It sits about 11 km east of central Honiara, the capital.

## Overview

HIR operates a single terminal on genuinely significant historical ground: its original airstrip, Henderson Field, was the central objective of the Battle of Guadalcanal (1942–43), one of the most pivotal and hard-fought campaigns of the entire Pacific War. The months-long struggle for control of this airfield marked a turning point that halted Japanese expansion in the South Pacific — history that modern travelers land directly on top of today.

## Terminals

HIR operates a single integrated terminal handling both regional and international traffic, including the full Solomon Airlines network connecting the country's many islands.

## Airlines

Solomon Airlines, the national carrier, operates its hub at HIR, running an extensive domestic network connecting Honiara to the Solomon Islands' many scattered islands, alongside regional international routes to Australia, Fiji, and Papua New Guinea. Given the country's dispersed geography, Solomon Airlines' network is essential infrastructure for inter-island travel.

## Getting There

- **Taxi:** **~100–150 SBD** to central Honiara, about 15–20 minutes.
- **Hotel shuttle:** Many hotels in Honiara offer pre-arranged airport pickup, often included with booking.

*Prices above are approximate and subject to change — always confirm current fares with your taxi or hotel.*

## Security and Passport Control Wait Times

- **Regional flights:** Security only for most inter-island and nearby Pacific routes. Typically **10–15 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **20–30 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **15–25 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **25–40 minutes** during peak international departure banks.
- HIR's relatively low passenger volume generally keeps processing quick outside of peak Solomon Airlines departure times.

## Parking

The terminal has adjacent short- and long-stay parking, with clear signage.

## Wifi

Free wifi is available in the terminal with a limited free session.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Regional flights:** 90 minutes before departure is typically enough.
- **International flights:** Arrive **2 hours** before departure, especially during peak Solomon Airlines departure banks.
- Given HIR's role connecting a widely dispersed island nation, inter-island connections can be tight — confirm your connection time when booking multi-leg domestic itineraries.

## Where to Eat

HIR has a good selection of Melanesian and Pacific cuisine alongside more standard airport dining options.

## Lounges

The **Solomon Airlines Lounge** is available for eligible business class passengers and elite status holders.

## Duty-Free Shopping

HIR's duty-free area includes genuinely local specialties — wood carvings and Pacific handicrafts — alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Layover Tips

- With 5 hours or more and a visa situation that allows entry, central Honiara and its WWII memorials and museums are reachable via taxi in about 15–20 minutes — a genuinely meaningful stop for anyone interested in Pacific War history.
- If staying airside, take a moment to consider the ground beneath the runway — Henderson Field's role in the Battle of Guadalcanal makes this one of the more historically significant airport sites in the Pacific.
- Given the Solomon Islands' dispersed island geography, most visitors continue onward by domestic flight rather than treating Honiara as a final destination — confirm your onward Solomon Airlines connection well ahead of arrival.
