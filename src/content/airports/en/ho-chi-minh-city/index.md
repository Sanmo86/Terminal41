Tan Son Nhat International Airport (IATA: **SGN**) is Vietnam's busiest airport and a major hub for both Vietnam Airlines and VietJet Air, with more than 40 million passengers a year. It sits about 7 km north of central Ho Chi Minh City — genuinely close by major-airport standards.

## Overview

SGN operates two terminals and carries a genuinely striking history: the site was once one of the busiest military airbases in the world during the Vietnam War. That history stands in sharp contrast to today's bustling civilian hub, now one of Southeast Asia's busiest airports, sitting just minutes from downtown Ho Chi Minh City.

## Terminals

- **Domestic Terminal:** Handles domestic flights across Vietnam Airlines' extensive network.
- **International Terminal:** Handles all international traffic, connected to the domestic terminal by walkway.

## Airlines

Vietnam Airlines, the national flag carrier, operates a major hub at SGN, with an extensive domestic network across Vietnam alongside growing international routes to Asia, Europe, and Australia. VietJet Air, Vietnam's largest low-cost carrier, also operates a significant base here. Together they make SGN one of the best-connected airports in Southeast Asia.

## Getting There

- **Taxi:** **150,000–200,000 VND** to central Ho Chi Minh City, about 20–30 minutes — highly dependent on the city's notoriously heavy traffic.
- **Grab:** Generally comparable in price to a taxi, about 20–30 minutes — widely used and recommended in Ho Chi Minh City.
- **Bus 109:** The cheapest option, about 40–50 minutes to central Ho Chi Minh City.

*Prices above are approximate and subject to change — always check current fares with the Grab app or official taxi counters.*

## Security and Passport Control Wait Times

- **Domestic flights:** Security only. Typically **20–30 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **35–50 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **25–40 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **45–65 minutes** during peak international departure banks.
- SGN handles a very high volume of both domestic and international traffic — budget real extra time during peak periods.

## Parking

Both terminals have extensive short- and long-stay parking, with clear signage.

## Wifi

Free wifi is available in the terminal with a time-limited free session.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Domestic flights:** Arrive **2 hours** before departure.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** before departure, especially during peak international departure periods.
- Given Ho Chi Minh City's notorious traffic, allow significant extra time for the airport transfer itself, not just terminal processing.

## Where to Eat

SGN has an excellent selection of Vietnamese cuisine alongside international chains, spread across both terminals.

## Lounges

- The **Vietnam Airlines Lotus Lounge** is available for eligible business class passengers, elite status holders, or paid access.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

SGN's duty-free area includes genuinely Vietnamese specialties — coffee and lacquerware — alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Layover Tips

- With 4 hours or more and a visa situation that allows entry, central Ho Chi Minh City is reachable via taxi or Grab, though traffic makes timing unpredictable — build in real buffer.
- If staying airside, SGN's dining and lounge options reflect Vietnam's strong culinary reputation — worth exploring even on a shorter connection.
- Given the airport's history and central location, a short city visit here offers a genuinely striking contrast between Ho Chi Minh City's fast-paced modern energy and its complex 20th-century history.
