Tunis-Carthage International Airport (IATA: **TUN**) is Tunisia's main international gateway and the primary hub of Tunisair, with more than 7 million passengers a year. It sits about 8 km northeast of central Tunis.

## Overview

TUN operates a single terminal and carries genuine historical weight in its name: Carthage, one of the great powers of the ancient Mediterranean world and a UNESCO World Heritage Site, is just a short trip from the airport. Founded by Phoenician settlers and later a major rival to ancient Rome, the ruins offer a genuinely striking glimpse into antiquity, remarkably close to a modern international airport.

## Terminals

TUN operates around a single terminal, handling both regional and international traffic, including the full network of Tunisair.

## Airlines

Tunisair, Tunisia's flag carrier, operates its principal hub at TUN, with an extensive network across North Africa and Europe, reflecting Tunisia's historic and ongoing ties to the Mediterranean region. That network makes TUN the essential gateway for travel throughout Tunisia.

## Getting There

- **TGM light rail:** **1.5–2 TND**, connecting toward central Tunis, about 20–30 minutes.
- **Taxi:** Metered, typically **15–25 TND** via the official taxi rank, about 15–20 minutes.

*Prices above are approximate and subject to change — always check current fares with the official taxi rank or TGM ticket counter.*

## Security and Passport Control Wait Times

- **Regional flights:** Security plus immigration for most North African and European routes. Typically **15–25 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **25–40 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **20–30 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **35–50 minutes** during peak international departure banks.
- TUN sees significant seasonal variation tied to Mediterranean tourism — expect longer waits during the summer high season.

## Parking

The terminal has adjacent short- and long-stay parking, with clear signage.

## Wifi

Free wifi is available in the terminal with a time-limited free session.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Regional flights:** Arrive **2 hours** before departure.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** before departure, especially during peak summer departure periods.
- Given the significant tourist season swing, add extra buffer time during the June–September high season.

## Where to Eat

TUN has a good selection of Tunisian cuisine alongside international chains, spread across the terminal.

## Lounges

- The **Tunisair Lounge** is available for eligible business class passengers, elite status holders, or paid access.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

TUN's duty-free area includes genuinely Tunisian specialties — olive oil and ceramics — alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Layover Tips

- With 4 hours or more and a visa situation that allows entry, the ruins of ancient Carthage are reachable via TGM light rail or taxi in about 20–30 minutes — a genuinely rare case where a layover can plausibly include real ancient history.
- If staying airside, TUN's dining reflects Tunisia's strong Mediterranean and North African food culture — worth exploring even on a shorter connection.
- Given the historical significance of the Carthage ruins, even a rushed visit during a longer layover is worth the effort if your schedule and visa situation allow it.
