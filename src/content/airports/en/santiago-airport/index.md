Arturo Merino Benítez International Airport (IATA: **SCL**) is Chile's busiest airport and LATAM Airlines' founding hub, handling more than 24 million passengers a year. It sits about 20 km (12 mi) northwest of central Santiago, with the Andes mountains rising dramatically just beyond the runways.

## Overview

SCL operates a large new international terminal, opened in 2022, which significantly modernized the airport's facilities. As LATAM's original hub — the airline traces its roots to Chile's national carrier LAN Chile — SCL anchors an extensive network across South America plus international routes to North America, Europe, and Oceania. The airport's setting is genuinely striking: the Andes rise sharply just to the east, visible from much of the terminal.

## Terminals

SCL operates around a single large, modern international terminal complex (opened 2022), consolidating what were previously more limited older facilities and significantly expanding capacity.

## Airlines

LATAM Airlines operates a major hub at SCL — the airline's historical roots trace back to Chile's LAN Chile — anchoring an extensive domestic network across Chile plus international routes throughout South America, North America, and beyond. That hub concentration makes SCL one of the best-connected airports for travel within Chile and to the wider Southern Cone region.

## How to Get There

- **Centropuerto bus:** **CLP 2,300–3,500**, the cheapest option, about 30 minutes direct to central Santiago.
- **Taxi:** **CLP 15,000–25,000** to the city center via the official taxi counter, about 25–35 minutes depending on traffic.
- **Uber:** Generally comparable in price to a taxi, about 25–35 minutes — widely used in Santiago.

*Prices above are indicative and can change — always check current fares with [Centropuerto](https://www.centropuerto.cl/) or your ride-hailing app.*

## Getting Through Security and Passport Control

- **Domestic flights:** Security only. Typically **20–30 minutes** off-peak, up to **35–50 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **25–35 minutes** off-peak, up to **45–65 minutes** during peak international departure banks.
- The new terminal's expanded security capacity has generally improved processing times compared to the airport's older facility.

## Parking

SCL has parking available at the new terminal, with clear signage for short- and long-stay options.

## Wifi

Free wifi is available in the terminal with a time-limited free session.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Domestic flights:** Arrive **2 hours** before departure.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** ahead, especially for LATAM's peak international departure banks.
- Santiago traffic can be unpredictable at rush hour — budget extra time for the drive to the airport during peak commute hours.

## Where to Eat

SCL has a good selection of Chilean cuisine alongside international chains, spread across the new terminal.

## Lounges

- **LATAM VIP Lounges** are available for eligible business class passengers, elite status holders, or paid access.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

SCL's duty-free zone includes a strong selection of Chilean wine, one of the country's best-known exports, alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Long Layover Tips

- With 4+ hours and a visa situation permitting entry, central Santiago and the Plaza de Armas are reachable by bus or taxi in about 25–35 minutes.
- If staying airside, request a window seat on your next flight out — the Andes view on departure from Santiago is one of the more memorable in South American aviation.
- Given the new terminal's scale, allow real time to reach your gate after security, especially during LATAM's peak connecting banks.
