Kotoka International Airport (IATA: **ACC**) is Ghana's main international gateway and the base of Africa World Airlines, with more than 3 million passengers a year. It sits about 9 km northeast of central Accra.

## Overview

ACC operates around Terminal 3, opened in 2018, and has taken on a genuinely distinctive role beyond typical business and tourist traffic: since Ghana's "Year of Return" initiative, the airport has become a major gateway for African diaspora heritage tourism, with visitors — particularly from the Americas and the Caribbean — tracing their ancestry back through Ghana's historic coastal slave forts and cultural sites.

## Terminals

ACC operates around Terminal 3 (opened 2018), the modern main terminal handling both domestic and international traffic, including Africa World Airlines' regional network.

## Airlines

Africa World Airlines, Ghana's leading carrier, operates its main base at ACC, with a regional network across West Africa. A wide range of international carriers also serve Accra directly from Europe, the Americas, and elsewhere in Africa, reflecting Ghana's growing role as a stable, accessible West African gateway — both for business and for the diaspora tourism the country has actively cultivated.

## Getting There

- **Taxi:** **80–150 GHS** to central Accra, about 20–30 minutes depending on traffic.
- **Uber / Bolt:** Generally comparable in price to a taxi, about 20–30 minutes — widely used and recommended in Accra.

*Prices above are approximate and subject to change — always check current fares with your rideshare app.*

## Security and Passport Control Wait Times

- **Regional flights:** Security plus immigration for most West African routes. Typically **20–30 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **35–50 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **25–35 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **40–60 minutes** during peak international departure banks.
- ACC has seen growing passenger volume in recent years — budget extra time during peak tourism season.

## Parking

The terminal has adjacent short- and long-stay parking, with clear signage.

## Wifi

Free wifi is available in the terminal with a time-limited free session.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Regional flights:** Arrive **2 hours** before departure.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** before departure, especially during peak international departure periods.
- Accra traffic can be heavy at certain hours — add extra time for the airport transfer.

## Where to Eat

ACC has a good selection of Ghanaian cuisine alongside international chains, spread across the terminal.

## Lounges

Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are available for eligible passengers, elite status holders, or paid access.

## Duty-Free Shopping

ACC's duty-free area includes genuinely Ghanaian specialties — kente cloth and cocoa products — alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Layover Tips

- With 4 hours or more and a visa situation that allows entry, central Accra is reachable by taxi or rideshare in about 20–30 minutes.
- If you're one of the growing number of travelers visiting Ghana for heritage or diaspora reasons, note that many onward journeys to the historic coastal forts (like Cape Coast and Elmina) start from Accra and take several hours by road — plan accordingly beyond just the airport transfer.
- Given Accra's growing tourism traffic, book accommodation and onward transport ahead during peak diaspora-tourism periods, particularly around Ghana's annual heritage-tourism events.
