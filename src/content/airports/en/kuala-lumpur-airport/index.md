Kuala Lumpur International Airport (IATA: **KUL**) is Malaysia's busiest airport and the primary hub of Malaysia Airlines, with more than 50 million passengers a year. It sits about 45 km south of central Kuala Lumpur.

## Overview

KUL operates across the Main Terminal and klia2 (the dedicated low-cost carrier terminal) and holds a genuinely unusual distinction among the world's major airports: the Main Terminal's Jungle Boardwalk is a real tropical rainforest garden built into the building itself, complete with actual trees and a walking boardwalk — a striking, functioning piece of biophilic design rather than a themed attraction.

## Terminals

- **Main Terminal:** Handles Malaysia Airlines and most full-service international carriers, and is home to the Jungle Boardwalk rainforest garden.
- **klia2:** The dedicated terminal for AirAsia and other low-cost carriers, connected to the Main Terminal by the KLIA Ekspres and an Aerotrain.

## Airlines

Malaysia Airlines operates its principal hub at KUL, with an extensive network across Asia, Europe, and Oceania as a member of Oneworld. AirAsia, one of Asia's largest low-cost carriers, operates its main base from klia2, giving KUL an unusually strong combination of full-service and budget connectivity across Southeast Asia.

## Getting There

- **KLIA Ekspres:** **55 MYR**, a direct non-stop train to KL Sentral in about 28 minutes — the fastest and most reliable option.
- **Taxi:** Typically **75–100 MYR** to the city via the official coupon counter, about 45–60 minutes depending on traffic.
- **Grab:** Generally comparable in price to a taxi, about 45–60 minutes — widely used and reliable in Kuala Lumpur.

*Prices above are approximate and subject to change — always check current fares with [KLIA Ekspres](https://www.kliaekspres.com/) or the Grab app.*

## Security and Passport Control Wait Times

- **Regional flights:** Security plus immigration for most ASEAN routes. Typically **15–25 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **25–40 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **25–35 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **40–60 minutes** during peak international departure banks.
- If connecting between the Main Terminal and klia2, budget real time for the transfer, which requires the Aerotrain or KLIA Ekspres.

## Parking

Both terminal areas have extensive short- and long-stay parking, with clear signage.

## Wifi

Free, unlimited wifi is available throughout the airport with no time limit.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Regional flights:** Arrive **2 hours** before departure.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** before departure, especially during Malaysia Airlines' peak departure banks.
- Given the distance between the Main Terminal and klia2, allow real time if your itinerary connects between the two.

## Where to Eat

KUL has an excellent selection of Malaysian cuisine, including a genuine hawker-style food court, alongside international chains, spread across both terminals.

## Lounges

- The **Malaysia Airlines Golden Lounge** is available for eligible business class passengers, elite status holders, or paid access.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

KUL's duty-free area includes Malaysian chocolate and batik textiles alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Layover Tips

- With 5 hours or more and a visa situation that allows entry, central Kuala Lumpur is reachable via the KLIA Ekspres in about 28 minutes.
- If staying airside, don't skip the Jungle Boardwalk in the Main Terminal — it's a genuinely unusual and calming way to spend layover time, real trees and all.
- If connecting between the Main Terminal and klia2, factor in the transfer time as its own step, not just part of general security buffer.
