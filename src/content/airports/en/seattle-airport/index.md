Seattle-Tacoma International Airport (IATA: **SEA**) is the Pacific Northwest's main gateway and Alaska Airlines' largest hub, with more than 52 million passengers a year. It sits about 23 km south of downtown Seattle.

## Overview

SEA operates a central terminal connected to satellite concourses and holds a genuinely valuable geographic advantage: its location gives it some of the shortest flight times from anywhere in the US mainland to Asia, thanks to great-circle polar routes that curve up over the north Pacific. That advantage has made SEA a critical Asia-Pacific gateway despite Seattle not being one of America's largest metro areas — a reflection of geography mattering as much as population.

## Terminals

SEA operates a Central Terminal connected by an underground train to satellite concourses (A, B, C, D, N, and S), handling the full scope of Alaska Airlines' hub operations alongside Delta's significant West Coast presence.

## Airlines

Alaska Airlines operates its largest hub at SEA, running an extensive network across the western United States, Hawaii, and increasingly international destinations. Delta Air Lines also maintains a major hub here, adding substantial domestic and international capacity, particularly to Asia given the airport's favorable polar-route geography.

## Getting There

- **Link light rail:** **~$3**, a direct line to downtown Seattle, about 35–40 minutes.
- **Rideshare:** **~$35–50** depending on traffic, about 25–35 minutes.
- **Taxi:** Metered, typically **~$40–55**, widely available, about 25–35 minutes.

*Prices above are approximate and subject to change — always check current fares with [Sound Transit](https://www.soundtransit.org/) or your rideshare app.*

## Security and Passport Control Wait Times

- **Domestic flights:** Security only. Typically **15–20 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **25–40 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **25–35 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **40–55 minutes** during peak international departure banks.
- Given SEA's significant Asia-Pacific international traffic, international departure banks can see notably longer lines than the domestic side.

## Parking

The terminal has extensive short- and long-stay parking, with clear signage. Booking online ahead of arrival is cheaper than the walk-up rate.

## Wifi

Free, unlimited wifi is available throughout the airport with no time limit.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Domestic flights:** Arrive **2 hours** before departure.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** before departure, especially during peak Asia-Pacific departure banks.
- Given SEA's role as a major transpacific gateway, international connections here can involve significant walking between satellite concourses — factor in real transfer time.

## Where to Eat

SEA has an excellent selection of Pacific Northwest cuisine and locally roasted coffee spread across its concourses — a fitting reflection of Seattle's coffee culture.

## Lounges

- The **Alaska Lounge** is available for eligible business class passengers, elite status holders, or paid access.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

SEA's shopping area includes genuinely Pacific Northwest specialties — local coffee and regional gifts — alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Layover Tips

- With 5 hours or more, downtown Seattle and Pike Place Market are reachable via Link light rail in about 35–40 minutes.
- If staying airside, SEA's local coffee roasters are a genuine step up from typical airport coffee — worth seeking out even on a short connection.
- Given SEA's role as a transpacific gateway, allow real time for the underground train between satellite concourses on international connections — the walk alone can take longer than expected.
