Presidente Juscelino Kubitschek International Airport (IATA: **BSB**) is the main gateway to Brasília, Brazil's purpose-built capital, and one of the country's busiest domestic hubs, with more than 17 million passengers a year. It sits about 11 km south of central Brasília.

## Overview

BSB operates a single terminal and serves one of the 20th century's most distinctive urban planning achievements: Brasília was built from scratch in the late 1950s as Brazil's new capital, designed by urban planner Lúcio Costa and architect Oscar Niemeyer. Famously laid out in the shape of an airplane or bird when viewed from above, the entire city center holds UNESCO World Heritage status.

## Terminals

BSB operates around a single terminal, handling both a very high volume of domestic traffic — given Brasília's central location and status as the political capital — and a growing number of international routes.

## Airlines

LATAM Brasil and GOL both operate extensive domestic networks from BSB, reflecting Brasília's role as a major connecting hub given its central position within Brazil. That connectivity makes BSB one of the busiest domestic airports in the country, alongside a smaller number of direct international routes.

## Getting There

- **Taxi:** **40–60 BRL** to central Brasília, about 15–20 minutes.
- **Uber:** Generally comparable in price to a taxi, about 15–20 minutes — widely used in Brasília.
- **Ônibus (bus):** A cheaper option, about 30–40 minutes to central Brasília.

*Prices above are approximate and subject to change — always check current fares with your rideshare app or local bus operator.*

## Security and Passport Control Wait Times

- **Domestic flights:** Security only. Typically **15–20 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **25–35 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **20–30 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **35–50 minutes** during peak international departure banks.
- BSB handles an exceptionally high volume of domestic connecting traffic given its central Brazilian location — budget extra time during peak political and business travel periods.

## Parking

The terminal has extensive short- and long-stay parking, with clear signage.

## Wifi

Free, unlimited wifi is available throughout the airport with no time limit.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Domestic flights:** Arrive **2 hours** before departure.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** before departure, especially during peak departure periods.
- Given BSB's role as a major domestic connecting hub, allow extra buffer if your itinerary involves a tight domestic-to-domestic transfer.

## Where to Eat

BSB has a good selection of Brazilian cuisine alongside international chains, spread across the terminal.

## Lounges

- The **LATAM VIP Lounge** is available for eligible business class passengers, elite status holders, or paid access.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

BSB's duty-free area includes genuinely Brazilian specialties — cachaça and gemstones — alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Layover Tips

- With 4 hours or more and a visa situation that allows entry, central Brasília and its UNESCO-listed modernist landmarks (the Cathedral, the National Congress) are reachable by taxi or bus in about 15–40 minutes.
- If staying airside, BSB's dining and lounges reflect Brazil's strong culinary reputation — worth exploring even on a shorter connection.
- Given Brasília's unique urban planning, even a short city visit offers a genuinely distinctive architectural experience unlike almost anywhere else in South America — worth the detour if your layover allows it.
