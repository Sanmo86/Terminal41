King Abdulaziz International Airport (IATA: **JED**) is one of the busiest airports in the Middle East and Saudia's principal hub, handling more than 40 million passengers a year. It sits about 20 km (12 mi) north of central Jeddah, and serves as the main air gateway to Mecca, Islam's holiest city, roughly 70 km away.

## Overview

JED's defining feature is its role as the primary gateway for Hajj and Umrah pilgrims traveling to Mecca — the airport's historic **Hajj Terminal** has an enormous tent-like fabric roof, one of the largest tensile membrane structures ever built, specifically designed to process millions of pilgrims during peak religious travel seasons. A newer main terminal now handles the bulk of scheduled commercial flights.

## Terminals

- **Terminal 1 (North Terminal):** The main terminal for scheduled Saudia and international commercial flights.
- **Hajj Terminal:** A separate, historic terminal used primarily during Hajj and Umrah seasons to process the enormous surge of pilgrim traffic — its tent-like roof is a genuine architectural landmark.

## Airlines

Saudia, Saudi Arabia's flag carrier, operates its principal hub at JED, connecting the Kingdom to an extensive domestic network plus international routes across the Middle East, Asia, Europe, and beyond. JED's role as the main gateway to Mecca also makes it one of the busiest airports in the world during the Hajj and Umrah pilgrimage seasons specifically.

## How to Get There

- **SAPTCO airport bus:** **SAR 20–30**, part of the public bus network, about 30–40 minutes to central Jeddah.
- **Taxi:** **SAR 60–100** to the city center, about 25–35 minutes depending on traffic.
- **Uber / Careem:** Generally comparable in price to a taxi, about 25–35 minutes — both apps are widely used and reliable in Saudi Arabia.

*Prices above are indicative and can change — always check current fares with SAPTCO or your ride-hailing app.*

## Getting Through Security and Passport Control

- **Domestic flights:** Security only. Typically **20–30 minutes** off-peak, up to **35–50 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **30–45 minutes** off-peak, up to **55–80 minutes** at peak — during Hajj and Umrah seasons specifically, processing times can run considerably longer given the sheer volume of pilgrim traffic, so build in significant extra time if traveling during those periods.
- Saudi Arabia introduced an eVisa system for tourism in 2019, in addition to separate Hajj/Umrah visa processes — check which applies to your trip before flying.

## Parking

JED has parking available at the main terminal, with clear signage for short- and long-stay options.

## Wifi

Free wifi is available in the terminal for travelers.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Domestic flights:** Arrive **2 hours** before departure.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** ahead — and significantly earlier during Hajj and Umrah seasons, when the airport handles an extraordinary surge in both passenger volume and dedicated pilgrim processing.
- If your trip coincides with Hajj or Umrah season, check with your airline about specific recommended arrival times, since normal guidance doesn't fully account for the scale of pilgrim traffic during those periods.

## Where to Eat

JED has a good selection of Saudi and broader Middle Eastern cuisine alongside international chains, reflecting the airport's role as a major gateway for both business travelers and religious pilgrims.

## Lounges

- The **Saudia Al Fursan** lounges are available for eligible business class passengers, elite status holders, or paid access.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

JED's duty-free zone includes distinctly regional specialties — Arabic perfumes and oud, and dates — alongside more standard international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands, making it a good last stop for souvenirs specific to the region.

## Long Layover Tips

- With a valid Saudi visa and a long enough layover, central Jeddah's historic Al-Balad district (a UNESCO World Heritage old town) is reachable by taxi or rideshare in about 25–35 minutes.
- If staying airside, the main terminal has standard lounge and dining options — but even a glimpse of the historic Hajj Terminal's tent-like roofline, if visible from your route through the airport, is worth pausing for.
- If you're traveling during Hajj or Umrah season, build in significantly more buffer time than usual at every stage — this is one of the highest-volume periods at any airport in the world.
