Wellington International Airport (IATA: **WLG**) is New Zealand's third-busiest airport and a key domestic hub for Air New Zealand, with more than 5 million passengers a year. It sits about 8 km southeast of central Wellington.

## Overview

WLG operates a single terminal and is known for two genuinely distinctive things: a dramatic, wind-affected approach that has made landing footage here famous online (Wellington is nicknamed "Windy Welly" for good reason), and a massive sculpture of the eagle Gwaihir carrying Gandalf hanging from the terminal ceiling — a nod to Weta Workshop, the Wellington-based special-effects studio behind the Lord of the Rings and Hobbit films.

## Terminals

WLG operates around a single integrated terminal, handling both domestic and international traffic, including Air New Zealand's extensive domestic network.

## Airlines

Air New Zealand operates a key domestic hub at WLG, with an extensive network connecting Wellington to the rest of the North and South Islands, alongside a smaller number of international routes to Australia and the Pacific. That network makes WLG an essential connecting point for travel around New Zealand given Wellington's central location between the two main islands.

## Getting There

- **Metlink bus:** **NZD 3.50–5**, a direct service to central Wellington, about 20 minutes.
- **Taxi:** Typically **NZD 25–35** to the city center, about 15 minutes.
- **Uber:** Generally comparable in price to a taxi, about 15 minutes.

*Prices above are approximate and subject to change — always check current fares with [Metlink](https://www.metlink.org.nz/) or your rideshare app.*

## Security and Passport Control Wait Times

- **Domestic flights:** Security only. Typically **10–15 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **20–30 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **20–30 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **35–50 minutes** during peak international departure banks.
- Given WLG's more moderate scale, processing is generally quick even during busier periods.

## Parking

The terminal has adjacent short- and long-stay parking, with clear signage for both options.

## Wifi

Free, unlimited wifi is available throughout the airport with no time limit.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Domestic flights:** **90 minutes** before departure is usually sufficient.
- **International flights:** Arrive **2.5 hours** before departure, especially during peak periods.
- Wellington's famously gusty conditions occasionally cause delays — build in a little extra buffer if you're connecting onward.

## Where to Eat

WLG has a good selection of New Zealand cuisine and strong coffee culture alongside international chains, spread across the terminal.

## Lounges

- The **Air New Zealand Regional Lounge** is available for eligible business class passengers, elite status holders, or paid access.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

WLG's duty-free area includes New Zealand wine and Weta Workshop film merchandise alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Layover Tips

- With 3 hours or more and a visa situation that allows entry, central Wellington is reachable via Metlink bus or taxi in about 15–20 minutes.
- If staying airside, don't miss the Gandalf-and-eagle sculpture in the terminal — it's a genuinely famous piece of airport art and easy to photograph even on a short connection.
- Given Wellington's notoriously windy approach, allow a little extra buffer in your connection times here, especially on blustery days.
