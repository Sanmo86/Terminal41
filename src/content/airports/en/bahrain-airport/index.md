Bahrain International Airport (IATA: **BAH**) is Bahrain's main airport and the primary hub of Gulf Air, one of the oldest airlines in the Middle East, with more than 10 million passengers a year. It sits about 7 km northeast of central Manama — genuinely close by Gulf-region standards.

## Overview

BAH operates a single large terminal, opened in 2021 to replace the older facility, and stands out for a simple but valuable reason: it's one of the closest major airports in the Gulf to the capital city it serves. As Gulf Air's home hub — an airline with roots stretching back to 1950, among the oldest in the region — BAH anchors an extensive network across the Middle East, South Asia, and beyond. Official figures from Bahrain's Civil Aviation Affairs [put 2024 traffic at nearly 14 million passengers](https://www.newsofbahrain.com/bahrain/108599.html), up from 13.3 million in 2023, driven partly by a sharp rise in transfer traffic.

## Terminals

BAH operates around a single new terminal (opened 2021), handling both regional and international traffic, including the full network of Gulf Air and its partner airlines.

## Airlines

Gulf Air, Bahrain's national carrier and one of the oldest airlines in the Middle East, operates its principal hub at BAH, with an extensive regional network across the Gulf and South Asia alongside international routes to Europe and beyond. That long-established network makes BAH a genuinely useful, less congested connecting option compared to some of the region's larger hubs.

## Getting There

- **Taxi:** **3–5 BHD** to central Manama via the fixed-rate counter inside arrivals, about 10 minutes — genuinely one of the fastest airport-to-downtown trips in the region.
- **Ride-hailing:** Generally comparable in price to a taxi, about 10 minutes. Uber and Careem both operate in Bahrain.

*Prices above are approximate and subject to change — always check current fares with the taxi counter or your ride-hailing app.*

## Security and Passport Control Wait Times

- **Regional flights:** Security plus immigration for most Gulf routes. Typically **15–20 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **25–35 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **20–30 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **35–50 minutes** during peak international departure banks.
- The new terminal's expanded capacity has generally improved processing times compared to the older facility.

## Parking

The terminal has adjacent short- and long-stay parking, with clear signage for both options.

## Wifi

Free, unlimited wifi is available throughout the airport with no time limit.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Regional flights:** Arrive **2 hours** before departure.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** before departure, especially during Gulf Air's peak departure banks.
- Given BAH's proximity to Manama, transfer time is genuinely less of a concern here than at most major Gulf hubs.

## Where to Eat

BAH has a good selection of Bahraini and wider Gulf cuisine alongside international chains, spread across the terminal.

## Lounges

- The **Gulf Air Falcon Gold Lounge** is available for eligible business class passengers, elite status holders, or paid access.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

BAH's duty-free area includes genuinely Bahraini specialties — pearls and dates — alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Layover Tips

- With 3 hours or more and a visa situation that allows entry, central Manama is reachable by taxi in about 10 minutes — one of the fastest airport-to-downtown trips anywhere in the Gulf.
- If staying airside, the new terminal's dining and lounge options are a genuine step up from the older facility it replaced.
- Given BAH's more moderate scale and lighter congestion than its larger Gulf neighbors, this is often a smoother connecting option if your itinerary allows routing through here.
