Incheon International Airport (IATA: **ICN**) is South Korea's main international gateway and Korean Air's principal hub, handling more than 66 million passengers a year — one of the busiest airports in the world. It sits about 48 km (30 mi) west of central Seoul.

## Overview

ICN operates two terminals and is consistently ranked among the world's best airports, largely thanks to genuinely unusual on-site amenities: an indoor ice skating rink, a casino, a spa, indoor gardens, and a golf course are all built into the terminal complex — turning what's normally dead layover time into something closer to a destination in itself.

## Terminals

- **Terminal 1:** A mix of international carriers including many foreign airlines and Asiana Airlines.
- **Terminal 2:** Korean Air's main hub terminal and its SkyTeam partners, opened in 2018 to add capacity.

## Airlines

Korean Air operates its principal hub at ICN, anchoring Terminal 2 with an extensive domestic network across South Korea plus international long-haul routes as a SkyTeam member. Asiana Airlines also maintains a significant presence in Terminal 1. That dual-carrier structure makes ICN one of the best-connected airports in Northeast Asia.

## How to Get There

- **AREX (Airport Railroad Express):** **KRW 4,150–9,500** depending on all-stop or express service, about 43–66 minutes to Seoul Station.
- **Taxi:** **KRW 55,000–70,000** to central Seoul, about 60–70 minutes depending on traffic — comfortable but the most expensive option given the airport's distance.
- **Kakao T:** Korea's main ride-hailing app, generally comparable in price to a taxi, about 60–70 minutes.

*Prices above are indicative and can change — always check current fares with [AREX](https://www.arex.or.kr/) or your ride-hailing app.*

## Getting Through Security and Passport Control

- **Domestic flights:** Security only. Typically **15–20 minutes** off-peak, up to **25–35 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **25–35 minutes** off-peak, up to **40–60 minutes** during peak international departure banks.
- ICN is renowned for efficient processing despite its massive passenger volume, reflecting years of consistently topping global airport service rankings.

## Parking

Both terminals have extensive parking, with clear signage for short- and long-stay options.

## Wifi

Free, unlimited wifi is available throughout the airport with no time limit.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Domestic flights:** **90 minutes** before departure is typically enough.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** ahead, especially for Korean Air's peak long-haul departure banks.
- Given ICN's distance from Seoul, build in extra time for the journey to the airport itself, not just the terminal processing.

## Where to Eat

ICN has an excellent selection of Korean cuisine alongside international chains, spread across both terminals.

## Lounges

- The **Korean Air KAL Lounge** is available across Terminal 2 for eligible business class passengers, elite status holders, or paid access.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available in Terminal 1.

## Duty-Free Shopping

ICN's duty-free zone is famous for Korean skincare and cosmetics, alongside ginseng products and the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Long Layover Tips

- With 6+ hours and a visa situation permitting entry, central Seoul is reachable via AREX express train in about 43 minutes.
- If staying airside, don't skip the ice rink, spa, or indoor gardens — ICN is genuinely designed to make a long layover an experience rather than dead time.
- Given ICN's distance from Seoul, weigh whether a full city trip is worth it versus enjoying the airport's own extensive facilities, especially on a shorter layover.
