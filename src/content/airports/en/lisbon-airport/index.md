Humberto Delgado Airport (IATA: **LIS**), commonly known as Lisbon Airport, is Portugal's busiest airport and the primary hub of TAP Air Portugal, with more than 35 million passengers a year. It sits about 7 km north of central Lisbon — genuinely close by major-airport standards.

## Overview

LIS operates two terminals and holds a genuinely distinctive position among European hubs: thanks to Portugal and Brazil's deep historic ties, TAP Air Portugal flies to more Brazilian cities directly than any other European airline, making Lisbon one of the smartest connecting points in Europe for onward travel to Brazil specifically. The airport's proximity to the city — just 7 km out — is a bonus on top of that network strength.

## Terminals

- **Terminal 1:** The main terminal, handling most international carriers including TAP Air Portugal's core operations.
- **Terminal 2:** Primarily low-cost carriers, connected to Terminal 1 by shuttle.

## Airlines

TAP Air Portugal, the flag carrier, operates its principal hub at LIS, with an extensive European network alongside its standout strength: more direct routes to Brazil than any other European airline, a legacy of the two countries' centuries-long historic and linguistic ties. That network depth makes LIS a genuinely useful routing choice for travel between Europe and Brazil specifically, beyond the airport's general European connectivity.

## Getting There

- **Metro (Red Line):** **1.65 EUR**, a direct line to central Lisbon in about 25 minutes — the cheapest and most reliable option.
- **Aerobus:** **4 EUR**, with multiple stops across the city center, about 30 minutes.
- **Taxi:** Typically **15–20 EUR** to the city center, about 15–20 minutes.

*Prices above are approximate and subject to change — always check current fares with [Metro de Lisboa](https://www.metrolisboa.pt/) or [Aerobus](https://www.aerobus.pt/).*

## Security and Passport Control Wait Times

- **Schengen flights:** Security only for most intra-EU/Schengen routes. Typically **15–20 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **25–35 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **25–35 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **40–60 minutes** during peak international departure banks, including TAP's busy Brazil-bound evening departures.
- LIS handles a high volume of both European and long-haul transatlantic traffic — budget extra time during peak banks.

## Parking

Both terminals have adjacent short- and long-stay parking, with clear signage. Booking online ahead of arrival is cheaper than the walk-up rate.

## Wifi

Free, unlimited wifi is available throughout the airport with no time limit.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Schengen flights:** Arrive **2 hours** before departure.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** before departure, especially during TAP's peak Brazil-bound departure banks in the evening.
- If connecting between T1 and T2, allow extra time for the inter-terminal shuttle.

## Where to Eat

LIS has a strong selection of Portuguese cuisine alongside international chains, spread across both terminals.

## Lounges

- The **TAP Air Portugal Lounge** is available for eligible business class passengers, elite status holders, or paid access.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

LIS's duty-free area includes genuinely Portuguese specialties — wine, canned fish, and cork goods — alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Layover Tips

- With 4 hours or more and a visa situation that allows entry, central Lisbon is reachable via Metro in about 25 minutes — one of the closer major-airport-to-downtown trips in Europe.
- If staying airside, T1 has the stronger combination of lounges and dining given TAP's core operations there.
- If your onward trip is to Brazil, LIS is genuinely worth considering as a routing hub — TAP's Brazilian network depth is hard to match from other European airports.
