Hamad International Airport (IATA: **DOH**) is Qatar's main gateway and one of the world's most highly rated airports, serving more than 50 million passengers a year. It's the home base of Qatar Airways and a major connecting hub between Europe, Africa, Asia, and Australia, sitting about 15 km southeast of central Doha.

## Overview

DOH operates as a single large terminal building divided into four concourses, purpose-built to handle Qatar Airways' extensive long-haul connecting traffic efficiently. It's consistently ranked among the world's best airports, known for a combination of striking architecture, art installations, and genuinely useful passenger amenities.

## Terminals

DOH's single terminal is organized into concourses:
- **Concourse A:** A mix of international carriers.
- **Concourses B, C, D:** Primarily Qatar Airways operations, reflecting its dominant presence as the hub carrier.

## Airlines

Qatar Airways dominates DOH as its home hub, operating one of the largest and most extensive long-haul networks in the world, connecting Doha to destinations across nearly every continent. This makes DOH one of the most useful connecting points globally, particularly for routes between Europe/the Middle East and Asia/Australia.

## How to Get There

- **Doha Metro:** **QAR 2** per trip (with a QAR 6 daily cap), the cheapest and most efficient option, about 20 minutes to the city center via the Red Line.
- **Karwa Taxi:** **QAR 40–60**, the official metered taxi service with a rank just outside arrivals, no booking required, about 15–25 minutes.
- **Uber / Careem:** Widely used and generally comparable in price to a Karwa taxi, about 15–25 minutes.

*Prices above are indicative and can change — always check current fares with [Doha Metro](https://www.qr.com.qa/) or your ride-hailing app.*

## Getting Through Security and Passport Control

- **Regional (GCC) flights:** Typically **15–20 minutes** off-peak, up to **25–35 minutes** during busy periods.
- **Long-haul international flights:** Typically **25–35 minutes** off-peak, up to **40–60 minutes** at peak — DOH is generally efficient by international standards, part of why it's so highly rated, though its huge volume of Qatar Airways connecting traffic can create busy periods around major banks.
- Given DOH's role as a major connecting hub, transfer passengers should follow signage carefully, as processes can vary depending on your itinerary.

## Parking

DOH has extensive parking directly at the terminal, well-organized given the airport's overall design focus on passenger experience. Rates and current options are best checked directly with Hamad International Airport.

## Wifi

Free, unlimited wifi is available throughout the airport with no time cap.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Regional flights:** **90 minutes** before departure is typically enough.
- **Long-haul international flights:** **3 hours** ahead is recommended, especially given the volume of connecting passengers during Qatar Airways' major banks.
- If you have time before your flight, build in extra time deliberately — DOH's art installations and amenities make it one of the more genuinely pleasant airports to spend time in.

## Where to Eat

DOH has a strong selection of Middle Eastern and international dining, generally upscale in line with the airport's overall design, spread across all four concourses. Options range from quick counters to full sit-down restaurants.

## Lounges

- **Qatar Airways Al Mourjan Business Lounge**, one of the largest and most highly rated airline lounges in the world, spanning a huge space with extensive dining and rest areas — available with eligible tickets or elite status.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

DOH's duty-free area is extensive and upscale, featuring gold and jewelry, perfumes, and dates alongside luxury fashion brands — reflecting Doha's position as a major shopping and luxury travel destination in its own right.

## Long Layover Tips

- With 5+ hours and a visa situation that allows entry (Qatar offers visa-free or visa-on-arrival access for many nationalities), central Doha and the Corniche are reachable via metro in about 20 minutes.
- If staying airside, don't miss the Lamp Bear sculpture and the airport's other art installations — DOH is genuinely worth exploring even without leaving the terminal.
- Qatar Airways offers a free transit hotel and stopover programs for eligible passengers with longer layovers — check your eligibility when booking, as it can turn a long connection into a comfortable rest stop.
