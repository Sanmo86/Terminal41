Charlotte Douglas International Airport (IATA: **CLT**) is one of American Airlines' largest hubs, with more than 53 million passengers a year. It sits about 11 km west of uptown Charlotte.

## Overview

CLT operates a single terminal with five concourses and holds a genuinely charming distinction among major US airports: long rows of wooden rocking chairs line its concourses, a simple feature that has become one of the airport's most beloved and photographed details. As one of American Airlines' busiest connecting hubs, CLT sees enormous volumes of passengers transferring between flights — and the rocking chairs have become a small, welcome way to pause amid that rush.

## Terminals

CLT operates a single integrated terminal building organized into five concourses (A through E), all connected airside, handling the full scope of American Airlines' hub operations along with other domestic and international carriers.

## Airlines

American Airlines operates one of its largest hubs at CLT, using it as a major connecting point for domestic travel across the eastern United States, alongside international routes to Europe, the Caribbean, and Latin America. That hub concentration makes CLT one of the busiest connecting airports in the country, even though Charlotte itself is a mid-sized city.

## Getting There

- **Rideshare:** **~$15–25** to uptown Charlotte, about 10–15 minutes.
- **Taxi:** **~$20–30**, widely available at designated stands, about 10–15 minutes.
- **CATS bus (Route 5X):** **~$2.20**, a budget option to uptown, about 25–30 minutes.

*Prices above are approximate and subject to change — always check current fares with [CATS](https://ridetransit.org/) or your rideshare app.*

## Security and Passport Control Wait Times

- **Domestic flights:** Security only. Typically **15–20 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **25–40 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **20–30 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **35–50 minutes** during peak international departure banks.
- Given CLT's role as a major connecting hub, security lines can back up significantly during American Airlines' peak connection banks — build in extra time if you're transferring.

## Parking

The terminal has extensive short- and long-stay parking, with clear signage. Booking online ahead of arrival is cheaper than the walk-up rate.

## Wifi

Free, unlimited wifi is available throughout the airport with no time limit.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Domestic flights:** Arrive **2 hours** before departure.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** before departure, especially during American Airlines' peak connecting banks.
- Given CLT's high volume of connecting traffic, factor in real time between concourses if you're transferring — some connections require a meaningful walk even though everything is airside.

## Where to Eat

CLT has a good selection of Southern American cuisine and regional chains spread across its five concourses.

## Lounges

- The **Admirals Club** is available for eligible business class passengers, elite status holders, or paid access.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

CLT's shopping area includes North Carolina crafts and NASCAR merchandise — a nod to the region's motorsport heritage — alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Layover Tips

- With 3 hours or more, uptown Charlotte is reachable via rideshare in about 10–15 minutes, though most passengers here are connecting rather than visiting the city.
- If staying airside, find one of the famous rocking chairs along the concourses — a genuinely pleasant way to spend a few minutes between flights.
- Given CLT's role as a major connecting hub, double-check your connecting concourse and allow real walking time — the distances between some gates are longer than they first appear.
