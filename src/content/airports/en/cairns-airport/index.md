Cairns Airport (IATA: **CNS**) is the main airport of Tropical North Queensland and a hub for both Qantas and Jetstar, with more than 4 million passengers a year. It sits about 7 km north of central Cairns — genuinely close by major-airport standards.

## Overview

CNS operates a single terminal and holds a genuinely important position for one of the world's most famous natural wonders: it's the real logistical gateway most Great Barrier Reef tour operators depart from, not the larger Australian cities travelers might assume. That role, combined with the airport's close proximity to the city, makes Cairns a legitimate reef-trip starting point rather than just a connection.

## Terminals

CNS operates around a single integrated terminal, handling both domestic and international traffic, including the combined networks of Qantas and Jetstar.

## Airlines

Qantas and Jetstar both operate significant services at CNS, together providing an extensive domestic network across Australia alongside international routes to Asia and the Pacific. That combined presence makes CNS a well-connected gateway for both business and the region's substantial reef and rainforest tourism industry.

## Getting There

- **Airport Shuttle:** **AUD 15**, a direct service to central Cairns, about 15 minutes.
- **Taxi:** Typically **AUD 20–25** to the city center, about 10 minutes — genuinely one of the shortest airport transfers of any major Australian tourism hub.
- **Uber:** Generally comparable in price to a taxi, about 10 minutes.

*Prices above are approximate and subject to change — always check current fares with your rideshare app or the airport shuttle counter.*

## Security and Passport Control Wait Times

- **Domestic flights:** Security only. Typically **10–15 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **20–30 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **20–30 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **35–50 minutes** during peak international departure banks.
- Given CNS's more moderate scale, processing is generally quick even during busier periods.

## Parking

The terminal has adjacent short- and long-stay parking, with clear signage for both options.

## Wifi

Free, unlimited wifi is available throughout the airport with no time limit.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Domestic flights:** **90 minutes** before departure is usually sufficient.
- **International flights:** Arrive **2.5 hours** before departure, especially during peak periods.
- Given CNS's smaller scale compared to Australia's major hubs, these times are generally more than sufficient outside of peak season.

## Where to Eat

CNS has a good selection of Tropical North Queensland cuisine alongside international chains, spread across the terminal.

## Lounges

- The **Qantas Club** is available for eligible business class passengers, elite status holders, or paid access.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

CNS's duty-free area includes Australian opals and tropical fruit wine alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Layover Tips

- With 4 hours or more and a visa situation that allows entry, central Cairns is reachable by shuttle or taxi in about 10–15 minutes.
- If your onward trip involves a Great Barrier Reef tour, book well in advance — reputable operators depart early and fill up, especially during peak tourist season.
- Given CNS's efficient, compact layout, this is a comfortable airport for a shorter connection compared to Australia's larger hubs.
