Ngurah Rai International Airport (IATA: **DPS**) is Bali's main gateway and a key hub for Garuda Indonesia's eastern Indonesia network, with more than 24 million passengers a year. It sits about 13 km south of Denpasar, close to the popular Kuta and Seminyak areas.

## Overview

DPS operates two connected terminals and holds a genuinely striking engineering distinction: its runway was extended out into the ocean on reclaimed land to accommodate larger modern jets, creating one of the most scenic final approaches of any airport anywhere — arriving flights pass low directly over open water just before touchdown. The airport's name also stands out: it honors I Gusti Ngurah Rai, an Indonesian national hero who led resistance against Dutch colonial forces and died in battle in 1946, rather than being named after a place, as most airports are.

## Terminals

- **International Terminal:** Handles all international arrivals and departures, including Garuda Indonesia's long-haul network.
- **Domestic Terminal:** Handles flights within Indonesia, connected to the international terminal by a short walkway.

## Airlines

Garuda Indonesia, the national flag carrier, operates a significant hub presence at DPS, connecting Bali to the rest of Indonesia's vast archipelago alongside international routes across Asia, Australia, and beyond. Citilink, Garuda's low-cost subsidiary, also runs an extensive domestic network from DPS, reflecting Bali's role as both a global tourist destination and a key domestic connecting point.

## Getting There

- **Blue Bird Taxi:** **100,000–200,000 IDR** depending on destination, metered and a widely trusted brand in Indonesia, about 15–30 minutes.
- **Trans Metro Dewata:** A low-cost public bus option with limited route coverage, about 30–45 minutes.
- **Hotel/resort shuttle:** Many resorts across Bali offer pre-arranged airport pickup, often included with booking.

*Prices above are approximate and subject to change — always check current fares with [Blue Bird](https://www.bluebirdgroup.com/) or your resort.*

## Security and Passport Control Wait Times

- **Domestic flights:** Security only. Typically **15–20 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **25–35 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **20–30 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **35–50 minutes** during peak international departure banks.
- DPS sees significant seasonal variation tied to peak tourist season (July–August and December–January) — expect longer waits during those windows.

## Parking

Both terminals have adjacent short- and long-stay parking, with clear signage.

## Wifi

Free, unlimited wifi is available throughout the airport with no time limit.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Domestic flights:** Arrive **2 hours** before departure.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** before departure, especially during peak tourist season.
- Given Bali's status as a top global destination, book ground transport in advance during peak season, when demand for taxis and shuttles is highest.

## Where to Eat

DPS has a strong selection of Indonesian and Balinese cuisine alongside international chains, spread across both terminals.

## Lounges

- The **Garuda Indonesia Lounge** is available for eligible business class passengers, elite status holders, or paid access.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

DPS's duty-free area includes genuinely Balinese specialties — local coffee (including kopi luwak), batik textiles, and silver jewelry — alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Layover Tips

- With 5 hours or more and a visa situation that allows entry (Indonesia offers visa-on-arrival for many nationalities), Kuta's beaches are reachable via taxi in about 15–20 minutes.
- If staying airside, take a moment during the descent or ascent to appreciate the runway's ocean-side setting — a genuinely rare engineering feature among the world's major airports.
- Given Bali's popularity, confirm ground transport arrangements ahead of time during peak season rather than relying on availability on arrival.
