Addis Ababa Bole International Airport (IATA: **ADD**) is one of the busiest and most important connecting hubs in Africa, serving more than 12 million passengers a year as the home base of Ethiopian Airlines, the continent's largest airline. It sits just 6 km (4 mi) from central Addis Ababa — remarkably close for a major international hub.

## Overview

ADD operates two terminals and functions as the primary connecting point for travel across Africa — more routes connect through Addis Ababa to other African destinations than through almost any other airport on the continent, thanks to Ethiopian Airlines' extensive pan-African network. Unlike most major hubs, ADD sits just a few kilometers from the city center, making it one of the most conveniently located big airports anywhere.

## Terminals

- **Terminal 1:** The airport's original terminal, handling a portion of domestic and regional flights.
- **Terminal 2:** The newer, larger terminal, serving as Ethiopian Airlines' main hub for both domestic and international long-haul routes.

## Airlines

Ethiopian Airlines, Africa's largest airline by fleet size and passengers carried, operates its exclusive hub at ADD, connecting dozens of African cities to each other and to an extensive international network spanning Europe, Asia, and the Americas. That hub concentration makes ADD the single most important connecting point for intra-African travel.

## How to Get There

- **Ride app:** **150–300 ETB**, Ethiopia's main ride-hailing app, about 15–20 minutes given the airport's short distance from the city.
- **Taxi (blue and white cabs):** **200–400 ETB**, negotiate the fare before getting in since meters aren't standard, about 15–20 minutes.
- **Hotel shuttle:** Many hotels in Addis Ababa offer free or low-cost airport pickup — worth checking when booking, especially for a short layover.

*Prices above are indicative and can change — always check current fares with your ride-hailing app or hotel directly.*

## Getting Through Security and Passport Control

- **Domestic flights:** Security only. Typically **15–20 minutes** off-peak, up to **25–35 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **25–35 minutes** off-peak, up to **40–60 minutes** during Ethiopian Airlines' peak connecting banks — as the hub for such an extensive network, ADD sees significant connecting traffic throughout the day.
- Ethiopia has one of Africa's most open visa policies, with e-visa and visa-on-arrival available for the vast majority of nationalities — a genuinely notable convenience if you're considering leaving the airport during a layover.

## Parking

ADD has parking available at both terminals, with clear signage for short- and long-stay options.

## Wifi

Free wifi is available in the terminal for travelers.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Domestic flights:** **90 minutes** before departure is typically enough.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** ahead, especially for Ethiopian Airlines' major connecting banks, which see high volumes of passengers transferring between African and international flights.
- Given ADD's role as Africa's primary connecting hub, if you're transferring between two Ethiopian Airlines flights, allow generous time even for a same-terminal connection during peak banks.

## Where to Eat

ADD has a solid selection of Ethiopian cuisine, including the option to experience a traditional coffee ceremony, alongside more standard airport dining options.

## Lounges

- The **Ethiopian Airlines Cloud Nine Lounge** is available for eligible business class passengers, elite status holders, or paid access, and is well regarded among African airport lounges.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

ADD's duty-free zone includes genuine Ethiopian specialties — coffee (naturally) and traditional textiles — alongside a smaller selection of standard international brands than some larger global hubs.

## Long Layover Tips

- With 4+ hours and a visa situation permitting entry (Ethiopia's e-visa system makes this easy for most nationalities), central Addis Ababa is genuinely reachable given the airport's unusually short 6 km distance from downtown.
- If staying airside, don't miss the chance to experience an Ethiopian coffee ceremony at the airport — a rare, authentic cultural experience available without ever leaving the terminal.
- Given ADD's role as the connecting hub for so much of Africa, double-check your connecting gate and terminal as soon as you land — Ethiopian Airlines' banked schedule means many flights depart in tight windows after major arrival waves.
