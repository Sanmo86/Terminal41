Queen Alia International Airport (IATA: **AMM**) is Jordan's main international gateway and the primary hub of Royal Jordanian, with more than 9 million passengers a year. It sits about 30 km south of central Amman.

## Overview

AMM operates a single terminal and serves as the essential gateway to one of Jordan's — and the world's — most famous sites: Petra, the ancient rock-carved city named one of the New Seven Wonders of the World, roughly a three-hour drive south. As Royal Jordanian's home hub, AMM also anchors an extensive regional network across the Middle East.

## Terminals

AMM operates around a single terminal, handling both regional and international traffic, including the full network of Royal Jordanian and its partner airlines.

## Airlines

Royal Jordanian, Jordan's flag carrier, operates its principal hub at AMM, with an extensive regional network across the Middle East alongside international routes to Europe, Asia, and North America. That network makes AMM a genuinely useful connecting point for travel through the Levant region.

## Getting There

- **Airport Express bus:** **3.5 JOD**, a direct service to central Amman, about 45 minutes — the cheapest option.
- **Taxi:** Typically **20–25 JOD** via the fixed-rate counter inside arrivals, about 35–40 minutes.

*Prices above are approximate and subject to change — always check current fares with the airport express counter or your taxi option.*

## Security and Passport Control Wait Times

- **Regional flights:** Security plus immigration for most Middle East routes. Typically **15–25 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **25–40 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **25–35 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **40–60 minutes** during peak international departure banks.
- AMM generally processes passengers efficiently despite handling a significant volume of tourist traffic headed to Petra and the Dead Sea.

## Parking

The terminal has adjacent short- and long-stay parking, with clear signage.

## Wifi

Free wifi is available in the terminal with a time-limited free session.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Regional flights:** Arrive **2 hours** before departure.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** before departure, especially during peak international departure periods.
- Given the distance to Petra and other tourist sites, plan your airport transfer with extra buffer if arriving on a tight tour schedule.

## Where to Eat

AMM has a good selection of Jordanian cuisine alongside international chains, spread across the terminal.

## Lounges

- The **Royal Jordanian Crown Lounge** is available for eligible business class passengers, elite status holders, or paid access.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

AMM's duty-free area includes genuinely Jordanian specialties — olive oil soap and Dead Sea cosmetic products — alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Layover Tips

- With 6 hours or more and a visa situation that allows entry, central Amman is reachable by taxi or airport express bus in about 35–45 minutes — though a genuine Petra day trip requires far more time and is best planned as a separate visit, not a layover activity.
- If staying airside, AMM's lounges and dining reflect Jordan's strong culinary tradition — worth exploring even on a shorter connection.
- Given Jordan's role as a gateway to Petra, the Dead Sea, and Wadi Rum, many travelers use AMM as the start or end point of a dedicated Jordan trip rather than a pure connection — plan accordingly if that's your itinerary.
