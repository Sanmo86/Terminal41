Muscat International Airport (IATA: **MCT**) is Oman's busiest airport and the primary hub of Oman Air, with more than 14 million passengers a year. It sits about 32 km northwest of central Muscat.

## Overview

MCT operates a single large terminal, opened in 2018 to replace the smaller original facility, and stands out among its Gulf neighbors for a genuinely distinctive design choice: while Dubai, Doha, and Abu Dhabi have each built taller, more futuristic glass mega-terminals, Muscat's new building deliberately leans into traditional Omani architectural motifs — a reflection of Oman's broader identity as a quieter, more traditional counterpoint to its flashier Gulf neighbors.

## Terminals

MCT operates around a single large terminal (opened 2018), handling both regional and international traffic, including the full network of Oman Air and its partner airlines.

## Airlines

Oman Air, the national carrier, operates its principal hub at MCT, with an extensive regional network across the Gulf and Indian subcontinent alongside growing international routes to Europe and Asia. That network makes MCT a genuinely useful, less congested alternative to the larger neighboring Gulf hubs for travel through the region.

## Getting There

- **Taxi:** **10–15 OMR** to central Muscat via the fixed-rate counter inside arrivals, about 30–35 minutes.
- **Mwasalat bus:** **0.5 OMR**, the cheapest option, about 40–50 minutes with a limited schedule.
- **Ride-hailing:** Generally comparable in price to a taxi, about 30–35 minutes.

*Prices above are approximate and subject to change — always check current fares with your taxi counter or ride-hailing app.*

## Security and Passport Control Wait Times

- **Regional flights:** Security plus immigration for most Gulf routes. Typically **15–20 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **25–35 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **20–30 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **35–50 minutes** during peak international departure banks.
- Given MCT's more moderate scale compared to its larger Gulf neighbors, processing is generally quick even during busier periods.

## Parking

The terminal has adjacent short- and long-stay parking, with clear signage for both options.

## Wifi

Free wifi is available in the terminal with a time-limited free session.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Regional flights:** Arrive **2 hours** before departure.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** before departure, especially during peak international departure periods.
- Given MCT's more moderate scale, these times are generally more than sufficient outside of peak hours.

## Where to Eat

MCT has a good selection of Omani cuisine alongside international chains, spread across the terminal.

## Lounges

- The **Oman Air Business Class Lounge** is available for eligible business class passengers, elite status holders, or paid access.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

MCT's duty-free area includes genuinely Omani specialties — frankincense and silver — alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Layover Tips

- With 4 hours or more and a visa situation that allows entry, central Muscat and the Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque are reachable by taxi in about 30–35 minutes.
- If staying airside, take a moment to actually look at the terminal's architecture — the traditional Omani design elements are a genuine departure from the glass-tower look of the region's bigger hubs, easy to miss on a rushed connection.
- Given MCT's more moderate scale and lighter congestion than its Gulf neighbors, this is often a smoother connecting option if your itinerary allows routing through here.
