Adelaide Airport (IATA: **ADL**) is South Australia's main airport and a hub for both Qantas and Virgin Australia, with more than 8 million passengers a year. It sits about 7 km west of central Adelaide — genuinely close by major-airport standards.

## Overview

ADL operates a single terminal and holds a genuinely enviable position: it's close to the city center, and beyond that, close to some of the most famous wine-growing regions on Earth. The Barossa Valley and McLaren Vale — both internationally renowned for their wine — are each under an hour's drive from the airport, making Adelaide a legitimate wine-tourism destination in its own right rather than just a stop on the way to somewhere else.

## Terminals

ADL operates around a single integrated terminal, handling both domestic and international traffic, including the combined networks of Qantas and Virgin Australia.

## Airlines

Qantas and Virgin Australia both operate significant services at ADL, together providing an extensive domestic network across Australia alongside a smaller number of international routes. That dual-airline presence makes ADL a well-connected gateway for both business and wine-tourism travel to South Australia.

## Getting There

- **JetBus / Adelaide Metro:** **AUD 5–6**, a direct service to central Adelaide, about 20 minutes.
- **Taxi:** Typically **AUD 20–25** to the city center, about 10–15 minutes — genuinely one of the shorter airport transfers of any major Australian city.
- **Uber:** Generally comparable in price to a taxi, about 10–15 minutes.

*Prices above are approximate and subject to change — always check current fares with [Adelaide Metro](https://www.adelaidemetro.com.au/) or your rideshare app.*

## Security and Passport Control Wait Times

- **Domestic flights:** Security only. Typically **10–15 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **20–30 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **20–30 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **35–50 minutes** during peak international departure banks.
- Given ADL's more moderate scale, processing is generally quick even during busier periods.

## Parking

The terminal has adjacent short- and long-stay parking, with clear signage for both options.

## Wifi

Free, unlimited wifi is available throughout the airport with no time limit.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Domestic flights:** **90 minutes** before departure is usually sufficient.
- **International flights:** Arrive **2.5 hours** before departure, especially during peak periods.
- Given ADL's smaller scale compared to Sydney or Melbourne, these times are generally more than sufficient outside of peak hours.

## Where to Eat

ADL has a good selection of South Australian cuisine alongside international chains, spread across the terminal.

## Lounges

- The **Qantas Club** is available for eligible business class passengers, elite status holders, or paid access.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

ADL's duty-free area includes a strong selection of South Australian wine, reflecting the region's international reputation, alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Layover Tips

- With 4 hours or more and a visa situation that allows entry, central Adelaide is reachable via JetBus or taxi in about 10–20 minutes.
- If you have more time and access to a rental car, the Barossa Valley or McLaren Vale wine regions are genuinely reachable in under an hour — a rare case where a layover could plausibly include an actual winery visit.
- Given ADL's efficient, compact layout, this is a comfortable airport for a shorter connection compared to Australia's larger hubs.
