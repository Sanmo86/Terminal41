Sharjah International Airport (IATA: **SHJ**) is a major gateway in the United Arab Emirates and the home base of Air Arabia, one of the busiest low-cost carriers in the Middle East, with more than 10 million passengers a year. It sits about 15 km southeast of central Sharjah, roughly 25 km from Dubai.

## Overview

SHJ operates a single terminal and holds a genuinely distinctive position among UAE airports: as Air Arabia's home base, it functions as one of the busiest low-cost carrier hubs anywhere in the Middle East. Sharjah itself is officially designated the UAE's cultural capital and is the country's only alcohol-free emirate, giving the whole experience — airport included — a noticeably different character from the more famous hubs in neighboring Dubai and Abu Dhabi.

## Terminals

SHJ operates around a single terminal, handling both regional and international traffic, including the full network of Air Arabia.

## Airlines

Air Arabia, one of the Middle East's largest and longest-established low-cost carriers, operates its principal hub at SHJ, with an extensive network of budget routes across the Middle East, North Africa, and South Asia. That network makes SHJ a genuinely important gateway for budget-conscious travel across the wider region, distinct from the full-service carriers based in Dubai and Abu Dhabi.

## Getting There

- **Taxi:** **30–40 AED** to central Sharjah via the fixed-rate counter inside arrivals, about 15–20 minutes.
- **Ride-hailing:** Generally comparable in price to a taxi, about 15–20 minutes. Careem and Uber both operate in Sharjah.

*Prices above are approximate and subject to change — always check current fares with the taxi counter or your ride-hailing app.*

## Security and Passport Control Wait Times

- **Regional flights:** Security plus immigration for most Gulf routes. Typically **15–20 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **25–35 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **20–30 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **35–50 minutes** during peak international departure banks.
- SHJ handles a very high volume of budget-airline traffic — budget extra time during Air Arabia's busiest departure banks.

## Parking

The terminal has adjacent short- and long-stay parking, with clear signage.

## Wifi

Free, unlimited wifi is available throughout the airport with no time limit.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Regional flights:** Arrive **2 hours** before departure.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** before departure, especially during Air Arabia's peak departure banks.
- Given SHJ's high volume of low-cost carrier traffic, don't underestimate security lines during busy periods — budget airlines here run high passenger volumes efficiently but the queues can still build up.

## Where to Eat

SHJ has a good selection of Emirati cuisine alongside international chains, spread across the terminal.

## Lounges

Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are available for eligible passengers, elite status holders, or paid access.

## Duty-Free Shopping

SHJ's duty-free area includes Arabic perfumes and dates alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Layover Tips

- With 3 hours or more and a visa situation that allows entry, central Sharjah is reachable by taxi in about 15–20 minutes.
- If staying airside, note that Sharjah's alcohol-free policy applies to the airport too — a genuinely different experience from a layover in Dubai.
- Given SHJ's proximity to Dubai (about 25 km), some travelers use Sharjah as a budget-friendly alternative arrival point for Dubai-area trips — factor in the extra transfer distance if that's your plan.
