Vancouver International Airport (IATA: **YVR**) is Air Canada's second-largest hub and Canada's main gateway to Asia-Pacific, handling more than 26 million passengers a year. It sits about 12 km (7 mi) south of downtown Vancouver.

## Overview

YVR operates domestic and international terminals connected internally, and is genuinely distinctive for its extensive collection of Indigenous Northwest Coast art displayed throughout the building — not a token gesture but a serious, deliberate curation that makes the terminal itself feel like a cultural destination. As Air Canada's Pacific gateway, it anchors an extensive network across Canada plus major routes to Asia.

## Terminals

YVR operates connected domestic and international terminal areas, with international arrivals and departures — including US-bound flights with border preclearance — handled in dedicated zones.

## Airlines

Air Canada operates its second-largest hub at YVR, anchoring an extensive domestic network across Canada plus a major international portfolio focused on Asia-Pacific routes, reflecting Vancouver's position as Canada's westernmost major gateway. That concentration makes YVR one of the best-connected airports in North America for travel to and from Asia.

## How to Get There

- **Canada Line SkyTrain:** **CAD 9–11**, a direct rail line running every few minutes to downtown Vancouver in about 25 minutes — fast, reliable, and unaffected by traffic.
- **Taxi:** Typically **CAD 35–45** to downtown, about 25–35 minutes depending on traffic.
- **Uber:** Generally comparable in price to a taxi, about 25–35 minutes.

*Prices above are indicative and can change — always check current fares with [TransLink](https://www.translink.ca/) or your ride-hailing app.*

## Getting Through Security and Passport Control

- **Domestic flights:** Security only. Typically **15–20 minutes** off-peak, up to **25–35 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control (US-bound flights clear US border preclearance here before departure). Typically **25–35 minutes** off-peak, up to **40–60 minutes** at peak.
- If you're flying to the US, remember you'll clear US customs and immigration at YVR itself — arrive at your gate on the US side already through both Canadian security and US preclearance.

## Parking

Both terminal areas have adjacent short- and long-stay car parks. Pre-booking online is cheaper than the walk-up rate.

## Wifi

Free, unlimited wifi is available throughout the airport with no time limit.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Domestic flights:** Arrive **2 hours** before departure.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** ahead, especially for US-bound flights that require full preclearance processing.
- If flying to the US, build in real extra time for preclearance specifically — it's effectively a second, separate check beyond standard security.

## Where to Eat

YVR reflects Vancouver's strong food scene well, with a good selection of Pacific Northwest cuisine and fresh seafood alongside international chains.

## Lounges

- The **Air Canada Maple Leaf Lounge** is available for eligible business class passengers, elite status holders, or paid access, reflecting Air Canada's major hub presence.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

YVR's duty-free zone includes Canadian specialties — ice wine and maple syrup — alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Long Layover Tips

- With 4+ hours and a visa situation permitting entry, downtown Vancouver and Stanley Park are reachable via the Canada Line in about 25 minutes.
- If staying airside, take time to genuinely look at the art around you — the collection here is considered one of the finest displays of Indigenous Northwest Coast art anywhere, not just an airport curiosity.
- If you're connecting to a US flight, factor preclearance into your connection time as its own distinct step, not just part of normal security.
