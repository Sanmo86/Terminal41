Perth Airport (IATA: **PER**) is Western Australia's main gateway and Australia's fourth-busiest airport, handling more than 14 million passengers a year. It sits about 12 km (7 mi) east of the Perth CBD, serving one of the most geographically isolated major cities in the world.

## Overview

PER operates four terminals and serves Western Australia's remote but economically significant capital — Perth is famously isolated, with the nearest comparably sized Australian city, Adelaide, more than 2,100 km away. That isolation shapes the whole character of the airport: flights to Perth from Australia's east coast take 4–5 hours, genuinely longer than many international routes from other Australian cities.

## Terminals

- **Terminal 1 & 2:** Domestic flights, including Virgin Australia's main Perth operations.
- **Terminal 3 & 4:** International flights and Qantas's domestic and international operations.

## Airlines

Qantas and Virgin Australia both maintain a significant presence at PER for domestic routes, connecting Western Australia to the rest of the country despite the vast distances involved. A growing roster of international carriers also connects Perth directly to Asia, the Middle East, and Europe, reflecting the city's role as a resource and mining industry hub with strong international business ties.

## How to Get There

- **Airport Line train:** **AU$3–5**, a direct rail line opened in 2022 connecting straight to the Perth CBD in about 20 minutes — fast, modern, and unaffected by traffic.
- **Taxi:** Typically **AU$35–45** to the city center, about 15–20 minutes.
- **Uber:** Generally comparable in price to a taxi, about 15–20 minutes.

*Prices above are indicative and can change — always check current fares with [Transperth](https://www.transperth.wa.gov.au/) or your ride-hailing app.*

## Getting Through Security and Passport Control

- **Domestic flights:** Security only. Typically **15–20 minutes** off-peak, up to **25–35 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control (or SmartGates for eligible passport holders). Typically **25–35 minutes** off-peak, up to **40–60 minutes** at peak.
- PER is generally efficient by international standards, and SmartGates speed up processing considerably for eligible travelers on arrival.

## Parking

All terminals have adjacent short- and long-stay car parks. Pre-booking online is notably cheaper than the walk-up rate.

## Wifi

Free wifi is available across the terminals with a time-limited free session.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Domestic flights:** **90 minutes** before departure is typically enough.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** ahead, especially for evening long-haul departures to Asia, the Middle East, or Europe.
- Given Perth's isolation, domestic connecting flights can be genuinely long-haul in their own right — treat a same-day connection through Perth from the east coast with real respect for the flight time involved.

## Where to Eat

PER reflects Western Australia's strong local produce well, with a good mix of local and international dining spread across all four terminals.

## Lounges

- **Qantas Club** lounges are available across the domestic and international terminals, accessible with eligible tickets, elite status, or membership.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

PER's duty-free selection includes Western Australian specialties — wine and the region's famous Broome pearls — alongside the usual international fashion and electronics brands.

## Long Layover Tips

- With 5+ hours and a visa situation permitting entry, central Perth and the Swan River foreshore are reachable via the Airport Line in about 20 minutes.
- If staying airside, both the domestic and international terminals have solid lounge and dining options for a longer wait.
- Given Perth's isolation, double-check total journey times carefully if connecting onward — a "domestic" leg from Perth can genuinely rival an international flight in duration.
