Tocumen International Airport (IATA: **PTY**) is Central America's busiest airport by a wide margin and Copa Airlines' exclusive hub, handling more than 18 million passengers a year. It sits about 24 km (15 mi) northeast of central Panama City.

## Overview

PTY operates two terminals and functions as the premier connecting point between North and South America — Copa Airlines built its entire route network around Panama's geographic position at the isthmus connecting both continents, marketing Tocumen as the "Hub of the Americas." Most passengers passing through PTY are connecting rather than starting or ending their trip in Panama, which shapes the airport's whole design around fast, efficient transfers.

## Terminals

- **Terminal 1:** Copa Airlines' main hub terminal, handling the bulk of connecting traffic between North and South America.
- **Terminal 2:** Opened in 2019, adding capacity for Copa's continued growth plus other international carriers.

## Airlines

Copa Airlines, Panama's flag carrier and a Star Alliance member, operates its exclusive hub at PTY, connecting dozens of cities across North, Central, and South America through Panama's central geographic position. That hub concentration makes PTY the single most important connecting point for travel between the two American continents, more so than any competing hub.

## How to Get There

- **Metrobus (public bus):** **$1.25–2.00**, the cheapest option, about 45–60 minutes to central Panama City.
- **Taxi:** **$25–35** to the city center, about 30–40 minutes depending on traffic.
- **Uber:** Generally comparable in price to a taxi, about 30–40 minutes — widely used in Panama City.

*Prices above are indicative and can change — always check current fares with your ride-hailing app or the airport's official taxi counter.*

## Getting Through Security and Passport Control

- **Domestic flights:** Security only. Typically **15–20 minutes** off-peak, up to **25–35 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **25–35 minutes** off-peak, up to **40–60 minutes** during Copa's peak connecting banks — as the hub for such a high volume of connecting traffic, PTY sees significant passenger flow concentrated around specific banked arrival and departure waves.
- If you're connecting between two Copa flights, follow the transfer signage carefully — PTY is specifically designed around fast connections, but peak banks can still get busy.

## Parking

PTY has parking available at both terminals, with clear signage for short- and long-stay options.

## Wifi

Free, unlimited wifi is available throughout the airport with no time limit.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Domestic flights:** **90 minutes** before departure is typically enough.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** ahead, especially for Copa's peak connecting banks.
- Given PTY's role as a connecting hub, if you're transferring between two Copa flights, allow generous time even for a same-terminal connection during peak banks.

## Where to Eat

PTY has a good selection of Panamanian cuisine alongside international chains, spread across both terminals.

## Lounges

- The **Copa Club** lounges are available for eligible business class passengers, elite status holders, or paid access, reflecting Copa's dominant hub presence.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

PTY's duty-free zone includes genuine Panamanian specialties — Panama hats, rum, and cigars — alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Long Layover Tips

- With 4+ hours and a visa situation permitting entry, central Panama City and the historic Casco Viejo district are reachable by taxi in about 30–40 minutes.
- If staying airside, both terminals have a solid mix of lounges and dining given Copa's hub presence.
- Given PTY's role as the primary North–South America connecting hub, double-check your connecting gate as soon as you land — Copa's banked schedule means many flights depart in tight windows after major arrival waves.
