Václav Havel Airport Prague (IATA: **PRG**) is the Czech Republic's busiest airport and the main base of Smartwings, with more than 17 million passengers a year. It sits about 17 km northwest of central Prague.

## Overview

PRG operates two terminals and serves as the gateway to one of Europe's most remarkable historic city centers. Prague's Old Town is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, widely considered among the best-preserved medieval old towns in Europe — largely spared the large-scale wartime destruction that reshaped many other European capitals during the 20th century.

## Terminals

- **Terminal 1:** Handles non-Schengen international flights.
- **Terminal 2:** Handles Schengen flights, connected to Terminal 1 by shuttle.

## Airlines

Smartwings, the largest Czech airline, operates its main base at PRG, with an extensive network across Europe and beyond. A wide range of other international carriers also serve Prague directly, reflecting the city's enduring popularity as one of Europe's most visited tourist destinations.

## Getting There

- **Airport Express bus + Metro:** **60 CZK** combined, about 45 minutes to central Prague — the most economical option.
- **Taxi:** Typically **500–700 CZK** via the official taxi rank, about 30 minutes.

*Prices above are approximate and subject to change — always check current fares with [Prague Public Transit (DPP)](https://www.dpp.cz/) or the official taxi rank.*

## Security and Passport Control Wait Times

- **Schengen flights:** Security only for most intra-EU/Schengen routes. Typically **15–20 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **25–35 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **25–35 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **40–60 minutes** during peak international departure banks.
- PRG sees significant seasonal variation tied to Prague's status as a major tourist destination — expect longer waits during summer.

## Parking

Both terminals have adjacent short- and long-stay parking, with clear signage. Booking online ahead of arrival is cheaper than the walk-up rate.

## Wifi

Free, unlimited wifi is available throughout the airport with no time limit.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Schengen flights:** Arrive **2 hours** before departure.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** before departure, especially during peak summer departure periods.
- Given the significant tourist season swing, add extra buffer time during the June–September high season.

## Where to Eat

PRG has a good selection of Czech cuisine alongside international chains, spread across both terminals.

## Lounges

- The **Airport Prague Lounge** is available for eligible business class passengers, elite status holders, or paid access.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

PRG's duty-free area includes genuinely Czech specialties — crystal glassware and Becherovka liqueur — alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Layover Tips

- With 4 hours or more and a visa situation that allows entry, central Prague and the Old Town Square are reachable via Airport Express bus and Metro in about 45 minutes.
- If staying airside, PRG's dining and lounge options are solid, though the real reason to come to Prague is the city itself — a longer layover is genuinely worth using for a quick Old Town visit if time allows.
- Given the heavy summer tourist season, book any onward transport or accommodation well ahead if traveling during peak months.
