Suvarnabhumi Airport (IATA: **BKK**) is Thailand's main international gateway and Thai Airways' principal hub, handling more than 50 million passengers a year. It sits about 25 km (16 mi) east of central Bangkok.

## Overview

BKK operates as a single large terminal complex, notable for its dramatic architecture and art — most strikingly, a massive golden relief mural depicting the Churning of the Milk Ocean, a well-known scene from Hindu mythology, dominating the main terminal hall. The airport's name, Suvarnabhumi, translates to "Golden Land." BKK [handled about 62 million passengers in 2024](https://www.thetraveler.org/bangkoks-suvarnabhumi-surges-as-new-aviation-power-in-asean/), making it the busiest airport in Thailand and the main driver of growth across Airports of Thailand's six-airport network.

## Terminals

BKK operates around a single large main terminal, with concourses handling both domestic and international flights, plus Thai Airways' extensive hub operations.

## Airlines

Thai Airways operates its principal hub at BKK, anchoring an extensive domestic network across Thailand plus international routes throughout Asia, Europe, and beyond as a Star Alliance member. BKK's central location within Southeast Asia also makes it one of the region's most important connecting points for other international carriers.

## How to Get There

- **Airport Rail Link (ARL):** **THB 15–45** depending on distance, running direct to central Bangkok's Phaya Thai station in about 30 minutes — the fastest option regardless of traffic.
- **Taxi:** **THB 300–500** plus tollway fees via the official taxi counter, about 30–45 minutes depending on traffic.
- **Grab:** Generally comparable in price to a taxi, about 30–45 minutes — widely used and reliable in Bangkok.

*Prices above are indicative and can change — always check current fares with the [Airport Rail Link](https://srtet.co.th/) or your ride-hailing app.*

## Getting Through Security and Passport Control

- **Domestic flights:** Security only. Typically **20–30 minutes** off-peak, up to **35–50 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **25–40 minutes** off-peak, up to **45–65 minutes** during peak international departure banks.
- BKK handles a very high volume of both business and tourist traffic — expect thorough but generally well-organized processing.

## Parking

BKK has parking available at the main terminal, with clear signage for short- and long-stay options.

## Wifi

Free wifi is available in the terminal with a time-limited free session.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Domestic flights:** Arrive **2 hours** before departure.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** ahead, especially for Thai Airways' peak international departure banks.
- Bangkok traffic can be notoriously heavy — budget extra time for the drive to the airport if not using the Airport Rail Link.

## Where to Eat

BKK has an excellent selection of authentic Thai cuisine alongside international chains, spread across the terminal. Inside the on-site Novotel, **Sala Thai** serves authentic Thai dishes, **Golden Village** covers Cantonese cuisine and daily dim sum, and **Kinsen** does Japanese teppanyaki — all open to travelers, not just hotel guests.

## Airport Hotel

The **Novotel Bangkok Suvarnabhumi Airport Hotel** is the only official on-site hotel, reachable via a covered, air-conditioned underground walkway about a 5-minute walk from the terminal — no need to go back through security to reach it. Beyond its Thai, Cantonese, and Japanese restaurants, it also has **The Square**, a 24-hour international buffet restaurant, genuinely useful for odd-hour arrivals or long layovers.

## Lounges

- The **Thai Airways Royal Orchid Lounge** is available for eligible business class passengers, elite status holders, or paid access.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

BKK's duty-free zone includes genuine Thai specialties — silk products and spices — alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Long Layover Tips

- With 6+ hours and a visa situation permitting entry, central Bangkok is reachable via the Airport Rail Link in about 30 minutes.
- If staying airside, take time to actually look at the golden mural in the main hall — it's a genuine work of art, not just decoration, and easy to walk past without noticing on a rushed connection.
- Given Bangkok's traffic, if you're heading into the city on a tight layover, the Airport Rail Link is far more reliable timing-wise than a taxi.
