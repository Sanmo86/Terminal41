Orlando International Airport (IATA: **MCO**) is one of the busiest airports in the United States and Southwest Airlines' largest focus city, with more than 57 million passengers a year. It sits about 10 km southeast of downtown Orlando, with the major theme park resorts a further 20–30 km southwest.

## Overview

MCO operates two terminal complexes and holds a genuinely distinctive character among major world airports: its traffic is overwhelmingly leisure travel rather than business, as the primary gateway to Walt Disney World, Universal Orlando, and the world's most-visited theme park resort complex. That skews everything about the airport's rhythm — more families, more first-time flyers, and one of the highest shares of vacation travel of any airport this size anywhere.

## Terminals

- **Terminal A/B:** The main terminal complex, handling the majority of domestic and international carriers.
- **Terminal C:** A newer terminal handling additional international and domestic capacity, connected by shuttle.

## Airlines

Southwest Airlines treats MCO as its single largest focus city, running an extensive domestic network from Orlando given the sheer volume of leisure travelers passing through. Delta, American, and United also maintain significant operations, alongside a wide range of international carriers serving Orlando directly given its status as a top global tourist destination.

## Getting There

- **Rideshare:** **~$25–40** to the International Drive theme park area, about 20–25 minutes.
- **Taxi:** **~$35–50**, widely available at designated stands, about 20–25 minutes.
- **Hotel/resort shuttle:** Many theme park resorts offer direct pickup, often included with booking, about 25–40 minutes depending on the resort.

*Prices above are approximate and subject to change — always check current fares with your rideshare app or resort.*

## Security and Passport Control Wait Times

- **Domestic flights:** Security only. Typically **15–25 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **30–45 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **25–35 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **40–60 minutes** during peak international departure banks.
- MCO sees strong seasonal variation tied to school holidays and peak theme park season — expect longer waits during those windows.

## Parking

Both terminals have extensive short- and long-stay parking, with clear signage. Booking online ahead of arrival is cheaper than the walk-up rate.

## Wifi

Free, unlimited wifi is available throughout the airport with no time limit.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Domestic flights:** Arrive **2 hours** before departure.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** before departure, especially during peak vacation season.
- Given MCO's heavy family and group travel, security lines can move slower than at business-traveler-heavy airports — build in a bit of extra buffer.

## Where to Eat

MCO has a solid selection of American chains and Florida-inspired dining spread across both terminals.

## Lounges

- The **Delta Sky Club** is available for eligible business class passengers, elite status holders, or paid access.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

MCO's shopping area leans heavily into theme park merchandise and Florida souvenirs, alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands — a reflection of just how many passengers are heading to or from the parks.

## Layover Tips

- With 6 hours or more, a quick trip to the theme park area is technically possible, though most of that time will go to transfers rather than an actual park visit — this isn't a realistic layover-to-park-day plan.
- If staying airside, MCO's dining and shopping already lean into the vacation mood — a decent preview of the trip ahead.
- Given how family- and group-heavy MCO's traffic is, security and bag-drop lines can run slower than the posted average during peak vacation weeks — build in real buffer time.
