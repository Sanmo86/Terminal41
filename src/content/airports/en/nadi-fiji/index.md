Nadi International Airport (IATA: **NAN**) is Fiji's main international gateway and Fiji Airways' hub, serving more than 2 million passengers a year as the essential connecting point for much of the South Pacific. It sits about 9 km (6 mi) from Nadi town, and about 20 km (12 mi) from the Denarau resort area.

## Overview

NAN is modest in passenger volume compared to major global hubs, but its role is genuinely outsized: Fiji Airways operates an extensive network connecting more than a dozen smaller Pacific Island nations — many with limited or no other international air access — through this single airport. For a huge portion of the South Pacific, Nadi is the only realistic gateway to the rest of the world.

## Terminals

NAN operates a single international terminal handling both Fiji Airways' regional network and long-haul international flights to Australia, New Zealand, North America, and Asia.

## Airlines

Fiji Airways, the national carrier, operates its hub at NAN, connecting Fiji to an extensive network of smaller Pacific Island nations plus long-haul routes to Australia, New Zealand, the United States, and Asia. That network makes NAN one of the most important — if not the single most important — aviation connecting points in the entire South Pacific region.

## How to Get There

- **Taxi:** **FJD 15–25** to Nadi town, about 10–15 minutes.
- **Resort shuttle:** Many hotels, especially around Denarau, offer shuttle services — pricing varies by property, about 20–40 minutes depending on destination.

*Prices above are indicative and can change — always check current fares with your hotel or a local taxi.*

## Getting Through Security and Passport Control

- **Domestic flights:** Security only. Typically **15–20 minutes** off-peak, up to **20–30 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **20–30 minutes** off-peak, up to **35–50 minutes** during peak departure banks.
- Given NAN's relatively modest overall size compared to major global hubs, processing is generally straightforward outside of peak periods tied to major international connections.

## Parking

NAN has parking available at the terminal for travelers arriving by car.

## Wifi

Free wifi is available in the terminal with a time-limited free session.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Domestic flights:** **90 minutes** before departure is typically enough.
- **International flights:** Arrive **2.5 hours** ahead, especially for Fiji Airways' long-haul departures to Australia, New Zealand, or beyond.
- Given NAN's essential role connecting smaller Pacific nations, some connecting itineraries through Fiji involve limited flight frequency — double-check your connection times carefully when booking.

## Where to Eat

NAN has a good selection of Fijian and broader Pacific cuisine, reflecting the airport's role as a genuine regional gateway rather than just a resort transit point.

## Lounges

- The **Fiji Airways Tabua Club** lounge is available for eligible business class passengers, elite status holders, or paid access.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

NAN's duty-free zone includes genuine Fijian specialties — kava and local crafts — reflecting the country's culture more directly than the standard international brand mix found at larger hubs.

## Long Layover Tips

- With 4+ hours and a visa situation permitting entry, Nadi town and the nearby Denarau resort strip are reachable by taxi in 10–40 minutes depending on destination.
- If staying airside, the Tabua Club lounge offers a comfortable option for a longer wait.
- Given NAN's role as the essential hub for so many smaller Pacific nations, double-check your onward connection well in advance — some island routes fly infrequently, and missing one can mean a genuinely long wait for the next option.
