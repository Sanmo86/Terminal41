Auckland Airport (IATA: **AKL**) is New Zealand's busiest airport and the country's main international gateway, sitting about 21 km south of central Auckland. It's the home base of Air New Zealand and the primary entry point for most visitors arriving in the country.

## Overview

AKL currently operates separate international and domestic terminal buildings, connected by a covered walkway or shuttle bus rather than being part of one structure — a genuinely major terminal integration project is underway, planned to combine them into a single building by 2028–29, which will eventually cut domestic-to-international transfer times to a five-minute indoor walk.

## Terminals

- **International Terminal:** All international arrivals and departures, including Air New Zealand's long-haul network.
- **Domestic Terminal:** Air New Zealand's domestic network across the country.

Until the integration project completes, always budget real transfer time between the two buildings if you're connecting between domestic and international flights.

## Airlines

Air New Zealand dominates AKL as its home hub, operating both an extensive domestic network and a growing international route map, including long-haul flights to North America, Asia, and the Pacific. A range of international carriers also serve the airport, particularly from Asia, Australia, and the Pacific Islands.

## How to Get There

- **SkyDrive Bus:** **NZ$20** flat fare, running every 30 minutes to the city center — the most straightforward option for most visitors.
- **AirportLink + Train:** **NZ$7.80** total, the cheapest option — take the AirportLink bus to Puhinui Station, then a standard Auckland train fare into the city, about 50–60 minutes total.
- **Taxi / Uber:** **NZ$55–95**, with no flat rate for taxis (the meter runs from pickup), about 25–35 minutes depending on traffic.

*Prices above are indicative and can change — always check current fares with [Auckland Transport](https://at.govt.nz/) or your ride-hailing app.*

## Getting Through Security and Passport Control

- **Domestic flights:** Security only. Typically **15–20 minutes** off-peak, up to **25–30 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control and New Zealand's notably thorough biosecurity/customs screening. Typically **30–45 minutes** off-peak, up to **50–70 minutes** at peak — arrivals in particular can take longer than at many other countries due to strict biosecurity checks.
- Declare all food, plant material, and outdoor equipment (hiking boots, camping gear) on arrival — undeclared items can result in on-the-spot fines even if unintentional.

## Parking

Both terminal areas have adjacent short- and long-stay car parks. Pre-booking online is cheaper than the walk-up rate, and off-airport parking options offer further savings for longer stays.

## Wifi

Free, unlimited wifi is available throughout the airport with no time cap.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Domestic flights:** **90 minutes** before departure is typically enough.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** ahead — New Zealand's biosecurity and customs processing can take longer than security alone, especially during peak tourist season (December–February).
- If connecting between domestic and international terminals, add extra transfer time beyond your security estimate, since the buildings are still separate for now.

## Where to Eat

Both terminal areas have a solid mix of New Zealand cafe culture — good coffee and local food chains — alongside international options. Selection is more limited than at larger global hubs but covers the essentials well.

## Lounges

- **Air New Zealand lounges** in both the domestic and international terminals, accessible with eligible tickets, elite status, or membership.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available, mainly in the international terminal.

## Duty-Free Shopping

AKL's duty-free area leans into New Zealand specialties — wine, manuka honey, and sheepskin products — alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Long Layover Tips

- With 5+ hours and a visa situation that allows entry, central Auckland is reachable via SkyDrive in about 30–45 minutes — the waterfront and Sky Tower area are worth a visit if time allows.
- If staying airside, the international terminal has the better mix of lounges and dining for a longer wait.
- Since the terminals are still separate buildings, if your layover involves switching between domestic and international, don't cut it close — allow real time for the walk or shuttle plus a second security screening.
