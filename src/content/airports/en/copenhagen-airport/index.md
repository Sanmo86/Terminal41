Copenhagen Airport (IATA: **CPH**) is Scandinavia's busiest airport and the primary hub of SAS (Scandinavian Airlines), with more than 30 million passengers a year. It sits about 8 km southeast of central Copenhagen — genuinely close by major-airport standards.

## Overview

CPH operates two connected terminals and stands out for a simple but genuinely valuable reason: it's one of the closest major airports in Europe to the capital city center it serves, with a direct Metro connection putting arriving passengers in central Copenhagen in about 14 minutes. As SAS's principal hub, CPH also anchors an extensive Scandinavian and European network.

## Terminals

CPH operates Terminal 2 and Terminal 3 as a connected complex, handling both Schengen and international traffic, including the full network of SAS and its Star Alliance partners.

## Airlines

SAS, the flag carrier of Denmark, Norway, and Sweden, operates its principal hub at CPH, with an extensive Scandinavian network alongside international routes across Europe, North America, and Asia as a member of Star Alliance. That hub concentration makes CPH the best-connected airport in Scandinavia for onward travel throughout the Nordic region.

## Getting There

- **Metro (M2):** **36–46 DKK**, a direct line to central Copenhagen in about 14 minutes — fast, frequent, and genuinely close given the airport's proximity to the city.
- **Train:** **30 DKK** to Copenhagen Central Station, about 15 minutes.
- **Taxi:** Typically **250–350 DKK** to the city center, about 20 minutes.

*Prices above are approximate and subject to change — always check current fares with [DOT (Din Offentlige Transport)](https://www.dinoffentligetransport.dk/) or your taxi option.*

## Security and Passport Control Wait Times

- **Schengen flights:** Security only for most intra-EU/Schengen routes. Typically **10–15 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **20–30 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **20–30 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **35–50 minutes** during peak international departure banks.
- CPH is regularly rated among the most efficient airports in Europe for security processing.

## Parking

Both terminals have adjacent short- and long-stay parking, with clear signage. Booking online ahead of arrival is cheaper than the walk-up rate.

## Wifi

Free, unlimited wifi is available throughout the airport with no time limit.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Schengen flights:** Arrive **2 hours** before departure.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** before departure, especially during SAS's peak departure banks.
- Given CPH's proximity to the city, factoring in transfer time here is genuinely less of a concern than at most major European hubs.

## Where to Eat

CPH has a strong selection of New Nordic cuisine alongside international chains, spread across its terminals.

## Lounges

- The **SAS Lounge** is available for eligible business class passengers, elite status holders, or paid access.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

CPH's duty-free area includes Danish design goods and Nordic chocolate alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Layover Tips

- With 3 hours or more and a visa situation that allows entry, central Copenhagen and Nyhavn are reachable via Metro in about 14 minutes — one of the fastest airport-to-downtown trips of any major European capital.
- If staying airside, CPH's dining and lounge options reflect Denmark's strong design and New Nordic food culture — worth exploring even on a shorter connection.
- Given the airport's efficiency and proximity to the city, CPH is a genuinely comfortable hub to build a same-day city visit around during a longer layover.
