Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International Airport (IATA: **MRU**) is the main gateway to Mauritius and the primary hub of Air Mauritius, with more than 4 million passengers a year. It sits about 48 km southeast of Port Louis, the capital.

## Overview

MRU operates a single terminal and serves an island with a genuinely unique claim in natural history: Mauritius is the only place the dodo bird ever existed. This flightless bird, having evolved with no natural predators, was driven to extinction within about a century of human contact beginning in the 17th century — and remains the island's most famous symbol to this day, despite no living person ever having seen one.

## Terminals

MRU operates around a single terminal, handling both regional and international traffic, including the full network of Air Mauritius.

## Airlines

Air Mauritius, the national carrier, operates its principal hub at MRU, with international routes connecting the island to Europe, Asia, Africa, and Australia. Given the island's status as a major honeymoon and luxury travel destination, MRU also sees a wide range of international carriers serving it directly during peak tourist seasons.

## Getting There

- **Taxi:** **800–1,200 MUR**, though pricing and travel time vary enormously depending on which part of the island your resort is on — some coastal resorts are close, others require a longer drive.
- **Resort shuttle:** Many resorts arrange their own airport transfer, often pre-booked as part of the accommodation package.

*Prices above are approximate and subject to change — always check current fares with your resort or the official taxi rank.*

## Security and Passport Control Wait Times

- **Regional flights:** Security plus immigration for most regional routes. Typically **15–25 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **25–40 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **20–30 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **35–50 minutes** during peak international departure banks.
- MRU sees significant seasonal variation tied to European winter holiday travel — expect longer waits during peak tourist season.

## Parking

The terminal has adjacent short- and long-stay parking, with clear signage.

## Wifi

Free wifi is available in the terminal with a time-limited free session.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Regional flights:** Arrive **2 hours** before departure.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** before departure, especially during peak international departure periods.
- Given MRU's status as a long-haul honeymoon and luxury destination, many flights are overnight — plan arrival timing around jet lag as much as the airport itself.

## Where to Eat

MRU has a good selection of Mauritian Creole cuisine — reflecting the island's blend of African, Indian, Chinese, and French culinary influences — alongside international chains, spread across the terminal.

## Lounges

- The **Air Mauritius Lounge** is available for eligible business class passengers, elite status holders, or paid access.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

MRU's duty-free area includes genuinely Mauritian specialties — local rum and traditional model ships, a well-known island craft — alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Layover Tips

- With 6 hours or more and a visa situation that allows entry, note that travel times to different parts of the island vary widely — confirm your specific destination's distance before planning any excursion.
- If staying airside, MRU's dining is a genuine introduction to Mauritian Creole cuisine, a distinctive blend of culinary traditions found almost nowhere else.
- Given how far-flung and long-haul most trips to Mauritius are, most travelers treat this as a dedicated destination rather than a stopover — plan accordingly if you're just passing through.
