Kigali International Airport (IATA: **KGL**) is Rwanda's main airport and the base of RwandAir, with more than 2 million passengers a year. It sits about 8 km east of central Kigali.

## Overview

KGL operates a single terminal and serves as the essential gateway to one of the world's great wildlife experiences: mountain gorilla trekking in Volcanoes National Park. Kigali itself adds to the appeal — the city is regularly ranked among the cleanest and safest capitals in Africa, known for its mandatory monthly community clean-up day (Umuganda) and one of the world's earliest bans on plastic bags.

## Terminals

KGL operates around a single terminal, handling both regional and international traffic, including RwandAir's growing network.

## Airlines

RwandAir, Rwanda's national carrier, operates its principal hub at KGL, with a growing regional network across Africa alongside international routes to Europe, the Middle East, and Asia. That expanding network has made KGL an increasingly important gateway for East and Central African travel.

## Getting There

- **Taxi:** **8,000–12,000 RWF** to central Kigali, about 15 minutes depending on traffic.
- **Moto-taxi:** A cheaper option, about 15 minutes — not recommended with heavy luggage.
- **Yego Cab app:** Generally comparable in price to a taxi, about 15 minutes — the local ride-hailing app.

*Prices above are approximate and subject to change — always check current fares with your taxi or ride-hailing option.*

## Security and Passport Control Wait Times

- **Regional flights:** Security plus immigration for most East African routes. Typically **15–25 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **25–40 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **20–30 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **35–50 minutes** during peak international departure banks.
- KGL generally processes passengers efficiently despite growing tourist traffic tied to gorilla trekking permits.

## Parking

The terminal has adjacent short- and long-stay parking, with clear signage.

## Wifi

Free wifi is available in the terminal with a time-limited free session.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Regional flights:** Arrive **2 hours** before departure.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** before departure, especially during peak international departure periods.
- If your trip involves onward travel to Volcanoes National Park, confirm your gorilla trekking permit timing well in advance — permits are limited and often booked months ahead.

## Where to Eat

KGL has a good selection of Rwandan cuisine alongside international chains, spread across the terminal.

## Lounges

- The **RwandAir Dream Lounge** is available for eligible business class passengers, elite status holders, or paid access.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

KGL's duty-free area includes genuinely Rwandan specialties — coffee and handwoven baskets — alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Layover Tips

- With 4 hours or more and a visa situation that allows entry, central Kigali is reachable by taxi in about 15 minutes.
- If staying airside, KGL's dining and lounge options reflect Rwanda's growing hospitality sector — worth exploring even on a shorter connection.
- Given Kigali's role as the gateway to gorilla trekking, if you're visiting Rwanda specifically for that reason, plan your permit and onward transport to Volcanoes National Park well ahead of arrival — this is not a same-day, layover-friendly excursion.
