Rafael Núñez International Airport (IATA: **CTG**) is the main gateway to Cartagena, Colombia's premier Caribbean coastal destination, with more than 4 million passengers a year. It sits about 3 km from the city's historic center — genuinely close by any major-airport standard.

## Overview

CTG operates a single terminal and offers one of the shortest airport-to-must-see-attraction transfers anywhere: the Ciudad Amurallada, Cartagena's UNESCO World Heritage walled old town, sits just minutes away. Centuries-old Spanish colonial fortifications, colorful balconied streets, and a genuine Caribbean coastal setting make this one of the best-preserved colonial port cities in the Americas.

## Terminals

CTG operates around a single terminal, handling both domestic Colombian routes and a growing number of direct international connections given Cartagena's popularity as a tourism destination.

## Airlines

Avianca, Colombia's flag carrier, operates an extensive network of flights connecting CTG to Bogotá and other major Colombian cities, alongside international routes to North America and the Caribbean. That connectivity, combined with Cartagena's tourism appeal, makes CTG one of Colombia's busiest airports relative to its size.

## Getting There

- **Taxi:** **20,000–35,000 COP** to the historic center via the fixed-rate counter inside arrivals, about 10–15 minutes.
- **Uber:** Generally comparable in price to a taxi, about 10–15 minutes.

*Prices above are approximate and subject to change — always check current fares with the taxi counter or Uber.*

## Security and Passport Control Wait Times

- **Regional flights:** Security plus immigration for most South American routes. Typically **15–20 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **25–35 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **20–30 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **35–50 minutes** during peak international departure banks.
- CTG sees significant seasonal variation tied to Caribbean tourism — expect longer waits during the December–March high season.

## Parking

The terminal has adjacent short- and long-stay parking, with clear signage.

## Wifi

Free wifi is available in the terminal with a time-limited free session.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Regional flights:** Arrive **2 hours** before departure.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** before departure, especially during peak international departure periods.
- Given CTG's compact scale, these times are generally more than sufficient outside of the busiest holiday periods.

## Where to Eat

CTG has a good selection of Caribbean Colombian cuisine alongside international chains, spread across the terminal.

## Lounges

- The **Avianca Sala VIP** is available for eligible business class passengers, elite status holders, or paid access.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

CTG's duty-free area includes genuinely Colombian specialties — coffee and emeralds, one of the country's signature exports — alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Layover Tips

- With 3 hours or more and a visa situation that allows entry, the walled city is reachable by taxi in about 10–15 minutes — genuinely one of the most convenient airport-to-landmark trips anywhere in the Americas.
- If staying airside, CTG's dining reflects Cartagena's strong Caribbean coastal food culture — worth exploring even on a shorter connection.
- Given how close the walled city sits to the airport, even a moderately long layover here can plausibly include a real (if rushed) walk through the historic center — just build in generous buffer for the return.
