Mohammed V International Airport (IATA: **CMN**) is Morocco's busiest airport and the primary hub of Royal Air Maroc, with more than 10 million passengers a year. It sits about 30 km southeast of central Casablanca.

## Overview

CMN operates two terminals and functions as Morocco's principal gateway to Africa, Europe, and beyond via Royal Air Maroc's extensive network. Its standout feature is genuinely practical: a train station is built directly into the terminal complex, putting arriving passengers straight onto Morocco's ONCF national rail network — including the Al Boraq high-speed line toward Tangier — without a shuttle bus in between.

## Terminals

- **Terminal 1:** Handles a mix of international carriers alongside Royal Air Maroc operations.
- **Terminal 2:** Additional capacity, connected to Terminal 1 via shuttle.

## Airlines

Royal Air Maroc, Morocco's flag carrier, operates its principal hub at CMN, with an extensive network across Africa — genuinely one of the most useful hubs on the continent for intra-African connections — alongside routes to Europe, North America, and the Middle East. That African network depth makes CMN a valuable connecting point for travel between Europe and francophone Africa in particular.

## Getting There

- **ONCF train:** **40–70 MAD**, direct to Casa Voyageurs station in about 35 minutes, with onward connections to Morocco's national and high-speed rail network — the standout option here.
- **Grand taxi (official):** **250–400 MAD** via the official taxi rank, about 30–40 minutes.
- **Hotel shuttle:** Some hotels offer their own transfer; check with your accommodation.

*Prices above are approximate and subject to change — always check current fares with [ONCF](https://www.oncf.ma/) or the official taxi rank.*

## Security and Passport Control Wait Times

- **Regional flights:** Security plus immigration for most regional routes. Typically **15–25 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **25–40 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **25–35 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **40–60 minutes** during peak international departure banks.
- Cross-terminal transfers between T1 and T2 add time — budget accordingly on connections.

## Parking

Both terminals have adjacent short- and long-stay parking, with clear signage.

## Wifi

Free wifi is available in the terminal with a time-limited free session.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Regional flights:** Arrive **2 hours** before departure.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** before departure, especially during Royal Air Maroc's peak departure banks.
- If connecting between terminals, allow extra time for the inter-terminal shuttle.

## Where to Eat

CMN has a good selection of Moroccan cuisine alongside international chains, spread across both terminals.

## Lounges

- The **Royal Air Maroc Lounge** is available for eligible business class passengers, elite status holders, or paid access.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

CMN's duty-free area includes genuinely Moroccan specialties — argan oil and leather goods — alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Layover Tips

- With 4 hours or more and a visa situation that allows entry, central Casablanca is reachable via the ONCF train in about 35 minutes — genuinely one of the more convenient airport-to-city rail links anywhere.
- If staying airside, T1 has the stronger combination of lounges and dining given the concentration of Royal Air Maroc operations there.
- Given Royal Air Maroc's strong African network, this is a genuinely useful connecting hub for travel between Europe and West/Central Africa specifically.
