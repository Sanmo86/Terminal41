Cape Town International Airport (IATA: **CPT**) is South Africa's second-busiest airport and the largest base for FlySafair, the country's biggest low-cost carrier, with more than 11 million passengers a year. It sits about 20 km southeast of central Cape Town.

## Overview

CPT operates a single integrated terminal handling both domestic and international traffic, and it is consistently rated the best airport in Africa in industry surveys. The airport's setting is genuinely dramatic — flights landing on a clear day pass directly beneath Table Mountain's flat summit, one of the most recognizable approaches anywhere on the continent.

## Terminals

CPT operates around a single integrated terminal complex, with domestic and international sections connected internally, handling FlySafair's extensive domestic network alongside international carriers serving Europe, the Middle East, and other African destinations.

## Airlines

FlySafair operates its largest base at CPT, running an extensive domestic network across South Africa as the country's biggest low-cost carrier. A wide range of international carriers also serve Cape Town directly from Europe, the Middle East, and elsewhere in Africa, reflecting the city's status as a major tourism destination in its own right, separate from Johannesburg's larger hub role.

## Getting There

- **MyCiTi Airport bus:** **ZAR 110**, a direct rapid-transit bus service to the city center, about 30 minutes.
- **Uber / Bolt:** Typically **ZAR 250–350** to the city center, about 25–30 minutes — widely used and generally recommended over hailing a street taxi.
- **Taxi:** Comparable pricing to rideshare via the official airport taxi rank, about 25–30 minutes.

*Prices above are approximate and subject to change — always check current fares with [MyCiTi](https://www.myciti.org.za/) or your rideshare app.*

## Security and Passport Control Wait Times

- **Domestic flights:** Security only. Typically **15–25 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **25–40 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **25–35 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **40–60 minutes** during peak international departure banks.
- CPT generally processes passengers efficiently given its more moderate scale compared to Johannesburg.

## Parking

The terminal has adjacent short- and long-stay parking, with clear signage for both options.

## Wifi

Free wifi is available in the terminal with a time-limited free session.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Domestic flights:** Arrive **2 hours** before departure.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** before departure, especially during peak international departure periods.
- Cape Town traffic can be heavier than expected during peak tourist season — allow extra time for the airport transfer.

## Where to Eat

CPT has a good selection of South African cuisine alongside international chains, spread across the terminal.

## Lounges

- The **Bidvest Premier Lounge** is available for eligible business class passengers, elite status holders, or paid access.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

CPT's duty-free area includes a strong selection of South African wine, one of the country's best-known exports, alongside local crafts and the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Layover Tips

- With 4 hours or more and a visa situation that allows entry, central Cape Town and the V&A Waterfront are reachable via MyCiTi bus or rideshare in about 25–30 minutes.
- If staying airside, request a window seat on your next departing flight if the weather is clear — the Table Mountain views on takeoff from Cape Town are among the most memorable in African aviation.
- Given Cape Town's status as a major tourism gateway in its own right, this is a genuinely good airport to build extra time around rather than treat as a pure connection.
