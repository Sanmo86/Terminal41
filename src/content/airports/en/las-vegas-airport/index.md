Harry Reid International Airport (IATA: **LAS**) is the primary gateway to Las Vegas and Southwest Airlines' major western hub, with more than 55 million passengers a year. It sits about 8 km south of the Strip.

## Overview

LAS operates two terminals and offers a genuinely unusual first impression: slot machines are scattered throughout the terminals and gate areas, meaning passengers can start gambling the moment they land, before they've even reached baggage claim. Renamed in 2021 from McCarran International in honor of the late US Senator Harry Reid, the airport remains one of the busiest leisure-travel gateways in the country, built entirely around a city that runs on tourism.

## Terminals

- **Terminal 1:** The main terminal, handling most domestic carriers including Southwest and American.
- **Terminal 3:** Handles international flights and some domestic carriers, connected by tram.

## Airlines

Southwest Airlines operates one of its largest hubs at LAS, running an extensive domestic network that reflects Las Vegas's status as a top US leisure destination. Allegiant Air, a low-cost carrier headquartered in Las Vegas, also bases significant operations here, connecting the city to a wide range of smaller domestic markets.

## Getting There

- **Rideshare:** **~$15–25** to the Strip, about 10–15 minutes.
- **Taxi:** Metered, typically **~$20–30**, widely available, about 10–15 minutes.
- **Hotel shuttle:** Many Strip hotels offer pickup, timing and cost vary by property, about 15–20 minutes.

*Prices above are approximate and subject to change — always check current fares with your rideshare app or hotel.*

## Security and Passport Control Wait Times

- **Domestic flights:** Security only. Typically **15–25 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **30–45 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **25–35 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **40–55 minutes** during peak international departure banks.
- LAS sees significant spikes tied to major conventions and events — check the Las Vegas events calendar before flying if you're on a tight connection.

## Parking

Both terminals have extensive short- and long-stay parking, with clear signage.

## Wifi

Free, unlimited wifi is available throughout the airport with no time limit.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Domestic flights:** Arrive **2 hours** before departure.
- **International flights:** Arrive **3 hours** before departure, especially during major convention weeks.
- Given how much of LAS's traffic ties to specific events and conventions, check what's happening in Las Vegas on your travel dates — major shows can significantly affect both security lines and ground transport availability.

## Where to Eat

LAS has a solid selection of American chains and local Las Vegas favorites spread across both terminals.

## Lounges

- **The Club at LAS** is available for eligible business class passengers, elite status holders, or paid access.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

LAS's shopping area leans into the city's identity with casino-branded souvenirs and Nevada gifts, alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Layover Tips

- With 4 hours or more, a quick trip to the nearer end of the Strip is technically feasible via rideshare, though a same-day round trip cuts it close given security lines on return.
- If staying airside, the terminal slot machines are a genuinely unique way to spend a layover, whatever your appetite for gambling.
- Given how event-driven Las Vegas traffic can be, build in extra time during major conventions or residencies — both security lines and rideshare availability get noticeably busier.
