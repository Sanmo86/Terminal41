Queenstown Airport (IATA: **ZQN**) is the main gateway to New Zealand's adventure tourism capital and a key hub for Air New Zealand, with more than 2 million passengers a year. It sits about 8 km northeast of central Queenstown.

## Overview

ZQN operates a single terminal and offers one of the most genuinely scenic approaches of any airport in the world, flying between the dramatic peaks of the Remarkables mountain range. The city it serves has a well-earned reputation: commercial bungee jumping as an actual business was born just outside Queenstown in 1988 at the Kawarau Gorge Bridge, and the town has been synonymous with adventure tourism ever since.

## Terminals

ZQN operates around a single integrated terminal, handling both domestic and international traffic, including Air New Zealand's network.

## Airlines

Air New Zealand operates a key hub at ZQN, with an extensive domestic network connecting Queenstown to the rest of New Zealand, alongside a growing number of direct international routes to Australia given the destination's international popularity. That connectivity reflects Queenstown's status as one of New Zealand's most important tourism gateways.

## Getting There

- **Taxi:** **NZD 30–40** to central Queenstown, about 10–15 minutes.
- **Connectabus:** A cheaper option, direct to central Queenstown, about 15–20 minutes.
- **Uber:** Generally comparable in price to a taxi, about 10–15 minutes.

*Prices above are approximate and subject to change — always check current fares with [Connectabus](https://www.connectabus.com/) or your rideshare app.*

## Security and Passport Control Wait Times

- **Domestic flights:** Security only. Typically **10–15 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **20–30 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International flights:** Security plus passport control. Typically **20–30 minutes** during off-peak hours, up to **35–50 minutes** during peak international departure banks.
- ZQN sees heavy seasonal variation tied to both ski season (June–September) and summer adventure tourism — expect longer waits during peak periods.

## Parking

The terminal has adjacent short- and long-stay parking, with clear signage for both options.

## Wifi

Free, unlimited wifi is available throughout the airport with no time limit.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Domestic flights:** **90 minutes** before departure is usually sufficient.
- **International flights:** Arrive **2.5 hours** before departure, especially during peak ski or summer seasons.
- Given ZQN's mountain-surrounded setting, weather can occasionally affect flight schedules — build in a little extra buffer if your travel dates coincide with winter conditions.

## Where to Eat

ZQN has a good selection of New Zealand cuisine alongside international chains, spread across the terminal.

## Lounges

- The **Air New Zealand Regional Lounge** is available for eligible business class passengers, elite status holders, or paid access.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available.

## Duty-Free Shopping

ZQN's duty-free area includes New Zealand wine and Merino wool goods alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Layover Tips

- With 3 hours or more and a visa situation that allows entry, central Queenstown is reachable via Connectabus or taxi in about 10–20 minutes.
- If staying airside, take a moment to actually notice the mountain views from the terminal windows — genuinely one of the more scenic airport settings anywhere.
- Given Queenstown's status as an adventure hub, many visitors extend even a short stopover into a full trip once they see the surrounding scenery — worth keeping some flexibility in your onward plans if possible.
