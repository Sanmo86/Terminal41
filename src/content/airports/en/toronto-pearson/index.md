Toronto Pearson International Airport (IATA: **YYZ**) is Canada's busiest airport by a wide margin, serving more than 47 million passengers a year. It's the primary hub for Air Canada and sits about 27 km northwest of downtown Toronto.

## Overview

Pearson operates two terminals, both large enough to handle the full range of domestic, transborder, and international traffic. One of its most distinctive features is a huge **US border preclearance facility** — one of the largest of its kind anywhere — which lets US-bound passengers clear American customs and immigration before boarding, arriving in the US as a domestic flight.

## Terminals

- **Terminal 1:** The larger of the two, home to Air Canada and its Star Alliance partners, along with several non-alliance carriers including Emirates.
- **Terminal 3:** Other international and domestic carriers.

Terminal 2 no longer exists in its original form — Pearson now operates with just Terminal 1 and Terminal 3, connected by the Link Train people mover.

## Airlines

Air Canada dominates Pearson as its largest hub, operating an extensive domestic network alongside major international routes to Europe, Asia, and beyond, based mainly in Terminal 1. WestJet and a range of international carriers also serve the airport.

## How to Get There

- **UP Express Train:** **$12.35** for a walk-up adult ticket (**$9.25** with a PRESTO card), the fastest and most reliable option at just 25 minutes direct to Union Station downtown, running every 15 minutes from about 4:55am to 1am.
- **Taxi (flat rate):** A regulated flat fare of **$60–$75** applies to downtown Toronto regardless of traffic, about 30–45 minutes for the roughly 28 km trip.
- **Uber / Lyft:** Generally priced at or below the taxi flat rate, with no surge pricing complications for most trips, about 30–45 minutes.

*Prices above are indicative and can change — always check current fares with [UP Express](https://www.upexpress.com/) or your ride-hailing app.*

## Getting Through Security and Passport Control

- **Domestic flights:** Security only. Typically **20–25 minutes** off-peak, up to **30–40 minutes** during busy periods.
- **International / US flights:** Security plus either Canadian passport control (for international) or full **US preclearance** (for US-bound flights) — a longer process than standard security since it functions as full US border processing. Typically **30–45 minutes** off-peak, up to **50–70 minutes** at peak, especially for US preclearance during busy transborder departure banks.
- Because US preclearance is essentially a full US customs and immigration process happening in Canada, treat a US-bound flight from YYZ with the same time buffer as a genuinely international departure — it is not a quicker "domestic-style" process just because you'll land in the US.

## Parking

Both terminals have extensive adjacent short- and long-stay car parks, plus more affordable off-site lots connected by shuttle. Pre-booking online is cheaper than the walk-up rate.

## Wifi

Free, unlimited wifi is available throughout Pearson with no time cap.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Domestic flights:** **90 minutes** before departure is typically enough.
- **International/US flights:** Arrive **3 hours** ahead, especially for US preclearance during peak transborder departure times, when the preclearance lines can be as busy as full international processing.
- Given Pearson's significant volume of connecting traffic, build extra buffer time into tight connections, particularly if you're switching between Terminal 1 and Terminal 3.

## Where to Eat

Both terminals have a solid mix of Canadian staples — including, inevitably, several Tim Hortons locations — alongside national and international chains and sit-down restaurant options. Terminal 1 has the larger overall selection.

## Lounges

- **Air Canada Maple Leaf Lounges** across Terminal 1, accessible with eligible tickets, elite status, or membership.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available across both terminals.

## Duty-Free Shopping

Pearson's duty-free selection includes Canadian specialties — whisky, maple syrup, and ice wine — alongside the usual international fashion, cosmetics, and electronics brands.

## Long Layover Tips

- With 5+ hours and eligibility to enter Canada, downtown Toronto is reachable via UP Express in just 25 minutes — the waterfront and CN Tower area are a short walk from Union Station.
- If staying airside, Terminal 1 has the widest range of lounges and dining for a long wait.
- If your onward flight is to the US, remember that preclearance means you'll go through full US border processing in Toronto — don't assume it's a quick formality, and budget accordingly.
