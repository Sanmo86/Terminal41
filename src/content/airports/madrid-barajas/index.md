Madrid-Barajas (IATA: **MAD**) is Spain's largest airport and the main hub for Iberia and the oneworld alliance in southern Europe. It sits about 13 km northeast of downtown Madrid and handles more than 60 million passengers a year, making it one of the busiest gateways between Europe and Latin America.

## Overview

MAD is spread across four terminals connected by a free internal shuttle (for airside connections) and a walkable — if long — landside layout. If you're connecting between Terminal 4 (T4) and Terminals 1-2-3 (T123), budget extra time: they are physically separated by several kilometers of taxiway.

## Terminals

- **Terminal 1, 2, 3 (T123):** Used mostly by non-Star Alliance international carriers and some domestic flights. Older, more compact, currently under phased renovation.
- **Terminal 4 (T4) and T4S (satellite):** The modern hub terminal for Iberia, American Airlines, British Airways, and the rest of the oneworld alliance. T4 and T4S are linked by an automated people mover (APM) that takes about 5 minutes.

Always check which terminal your flight departs from — a same-airport "connection" between T123 and T4 can take 30-45 minutes including the shuttle bus ride.

## Airlines

Iberia dominates MAD as its home hub, alongside joint-venture partners American Airlines and British Airways. Other major operators include Air Europa, Vueling, Ryanair, LATAM, Aeroméxico, and most major European flag carriers. This makes MAD one of the best-connected airports in Europe for flights to Latin America.

## How to Get There

- **Metro:** Line 8 (pink line) connects T4 and T123 directly to central Madrid (Nuevos Ministerios) in about 15 minutes. Cheapest option, runs from ~06:00 to ~01:30.
- **Cercanías train:** Line C-1 links T4 to Chamartín and Atocha stations.
- **Airport Express Bus:** 24-hour bus service to Atocha and Cibeles, useful when the metro is closed overnight.
- **Taxi:** A flat rate applies between the airport and anywhere inside the M-30 ring road — ask the driver to confirm it's being applied.
- **Ride-hailing:** Uber and Cabify operate from designated pickup zones outside each terminal.

## Parking

MAD has both short- and long-stay car parks at every terminal. For stays longer than 3-4 days, off-airport park-and-ride lots (with shuttle transfer) are usually significantly cheaper than the official terminal car parks — worth comparing before you fly.

## Wifi

Free wifi is available throughout the airport for the first 15-30 minutes; after that, session limits and speed vary. If you need reliable connectivity for work, an eSIM activated before arrival is the more dependable option.

## Best Times to Arrive

- **Domestic/Schengen flights:** 90 minutes before departure is generally enough outside peak hours.
- **International flights (non-Schengen):** Arrive at least 3 hours early — passport control queues at T4 and T123 can back up significantly during morning and early-evening banks of departures, especially in summer.
- Avoid the 06:00-08:00 and 18:00-20:00 waves if your itinerary lets you choose a flight time.

## Where to Eat

T4 has the strongest food selection, ranging from quick tapas bars to sit-down restaurants near the boarding gates. Post-security options include Spanish chains serving jamón, tortilla, and coffee, plus international fast food. Prices run noticeably higher than downtown Madrid, as is typical for large hub airports.

## Lounges

- **Iberia VIP Lounges** in T4 and T4S — accessible with business class tickets, Oneworld Emerald/Sapphire status, or paid day-passes.
- Several **Priority Pass**-affiliated lounges are also available across terminals, useful if you hold a travel credit card with lounge access included.

## Duty-Free Shopping

T4's departures area has a large duty-free zone with Spanish gourmet products (jamón ibérico, wine, olive oil), fashion, and perfume — a good last stop for Spanish souvenirs. T123 has a smaller but still solid selection.

## Long Layover Tips

- If your layover is 6+ hours and you hold a Schengen-eligible passport, consider a quick trip into Madrid via metro — the city center is roughly 30-40 minutes door-to-door from T4.
- If staying airside, T4's departure lounge has more comfortable seating and better food options than T123.
- Showers and rest areas are available in the priority lounges; there is no free public sleep area, so a lounge day-pass is worth it for overnight layovers.
